I have finished 'Shruti Saara Samuddharanam' classes. Now he will continue

DurvAsanA PratIkAra Dashakam is a work of ten verses by Vidyaranya Swami,
the Jagadguru of Sringeri Saradha Peetam, who has authored many Vedantic
texts including Panchadasi, Anubhutiprakasha etc. Vasana means a habit, a
tendency or an inclination. DurvAsanA means unhealthy habit or wrong
inclination. These DurvAsanAs are the hurdles to overcome for a mumukshu
seeking AtmajnAnam. Even the one who has completed Shravanam and Mananam
successfully has to overcome these DurvAsanAs to get established in Self
knowledge. PratIkAra means remedy or therapy. This work lists various types
of DurvAsanAs along with their remedy.
