Shruti Saara Samuddharanam means extracting the essence of the Shruti /
Veda. It is the major work of Totakaacharya who is traditionally considered
as one of the four principal disciples of Aadi Shankaraachaarya. The text
consists of 179 verses mostly in totaka chandas (meter), which bring out
the essential meaning of the Upanishads. The teaching is presented in the
form of a dialogue between a student and guru.

Om tat sat
