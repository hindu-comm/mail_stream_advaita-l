A compilation from the book ‘Sridakshinamurthistotram Vol.1 authored by Ved
Sri D.S.Subbaramaiya, published by the Sringeri Sharada Peetham.  This
compilation has 14 pages.

The schools cited here are: Sankhya, Yoga, Nyaya-Vaisheshika, Purvamimamsa,
Dharmashastra, Agamas (Shaiva and Pancharatra), Shakta tantra, Vyakarana
and a summing up from the Suta Samhita.
