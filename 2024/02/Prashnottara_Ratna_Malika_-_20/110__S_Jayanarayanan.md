Q105. कस्मात्सिद्धिः?
>From where perfection comes?
A105. तपसः |
>From austerity.


Q106. बुद्धिः क्व नु?
Where is intelligence found?
A106. भूसरे ।
In brahmanas.


Q107. कुतोबुधिः?
Where does intelligence come from?
A107. वृद्धोपसेवया |
>From constantly serving the elders.


Q108. के व्रिद्धा:
Who are the elders?
A108. ये धर्मतत्त्वज्ञा: |
Those who know dharma.


Q109. संभावितस्य मरणादधिकं किं?
What is more painful than death, for a respectable person?
A109. दुर्यशो भवति ।
Getting a bad reputation.


Q110. लोके सुखी भवेत् को?
Who will find happiness in this world?
A110. धनवान् |
One who is wealthy.


Q111. धनमपि च किं?
What is this wealth?
A111. यतश्चेष्टं |
That which meets our needs.
