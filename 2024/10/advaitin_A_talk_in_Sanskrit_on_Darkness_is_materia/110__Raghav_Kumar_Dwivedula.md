> How the text distinguishes between vishaya and vishayi is not as two

"Commonly not taking X to be a thing" is no guarantee that it is not a
thing.

The only positive argument made out by all of those who postulate that
darkness is absence of light, is, "who commonly takes darkness to be a
thing?"
