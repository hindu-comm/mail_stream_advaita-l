The above observation purportedly made by Sri SSS is not based on the
facts. Here is shown just one example to demonstrate that:

न त्वेवाहं जातु नासं न त्वं नेमे जनाधिपाः।
न चैव न भविष्यामः सर्वे वयमतः परम्।। 2.12।।

But certainly (it is) not (a fact) that I did not exist at any time; nor
you, nor these rulers of men. And surely it is not that we all shall cease
to exist after this.

For this Shankara's commentary at the end is:

*देहेभेदानुवृत्त्या बहुवचनं नात्मभेदाभिप्रायेण।।*

The plural number (in we) is used following the diversity of the bodies,
but not in the sense of the multiplicity of the Self.

Here is Jayatirtha's commentary to the same verse, based on Madhva:
where he cites Shankara's commentary above:

अत्र भगवता जीवानां परस्परमीश्वराच्च भेदे प्रतिपादितेऽपि *बहुवचनं
शरीरापेक्षया न त्वात्मापेक्षयेति वदतो *. भविष्यत्युत्तरम्।

Vedanta deshika (Ramanuja):

*देहभेदाभिप्रायेण बहुवचनम् नात्मभेदाभिप्रायेण इतिशङ्करोक्तं दूषयति
पारमार्थिकेति।*

The corresponding bhashya vaakya of Ramanuja that Deshika refers to is:

द्विचन्द्रज्ञानादौ तु चन्द्रैकत्वज्ञानेन पारमार्थिकतिमिरादिदोषस्य
द्विचन्द्रज्ञानहेतोः अविनष्टत्वाद् बाधितानुवृत्तिः युक्ता।

Thus the Ramanuja and Madhwa schools have taken the very vaakyas of
Shankara for refutation.  The above is just one example out of many from
the Bh.Gita itself.
