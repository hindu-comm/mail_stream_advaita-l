Namaste Abhishek ji

You have at least some free will in any situation.(Even after granting that
our free will is severely buffeted and conditioned by our past karmas and
their consequent vasanas.  incidentally all prArabdha is created by past
use of free will ).

Lastly if we use whatever little free will we have at the present moment,
then the free will grows stronger. In other words, it will be start to less
and less under the limitations of vasanas.


Om
