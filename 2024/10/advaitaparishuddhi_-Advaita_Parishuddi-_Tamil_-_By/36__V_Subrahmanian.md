This text lays bare the infallibility of Advaita in the face of the
objections raised by Sri Vedanta Desika in his Shatadushani.  Sri Sastrigal
explains in Tamil, in the first few minutes, the background relevance of
this book and its utility to the followers of Advaita. The text reveals
that the objections raised do not apply to Advaita.
