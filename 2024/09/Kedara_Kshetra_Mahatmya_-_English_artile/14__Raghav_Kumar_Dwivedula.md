Namaste Subbu ji
Sri Shankara Bhagavatpada is said to have completed his earthly sojourn in
Kedara. That is likely to be the only reason why there is a special
prohibition that (sectarian) vaiShNavas should not visit Kedara! And there
does not seem to be such a distinct prohibition for other shiva kshetras
presumably because Shiva is the greatest Vaishnava.

Thankfully such ridiculous prohibitions are ignored by most Madhva
followers today.

Om
Raghav
