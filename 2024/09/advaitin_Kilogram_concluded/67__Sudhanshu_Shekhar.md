Namaste Bhaskar prabhu ji.

I would just make one correction.

//So, mUlAvidyA is like that blindfold, which is capable of veiling
Brahman.//

Actually, the statement is not correct. AvaraNa is defined not as avidyA,
but as avidyA-chit-sambandha, which is avidyA-prayukta. So, it is
avidyA-vyApya but not avidyA itself.

To that extent the previous post may be modified.

Regards.
Sudhanshu Shekhar.
