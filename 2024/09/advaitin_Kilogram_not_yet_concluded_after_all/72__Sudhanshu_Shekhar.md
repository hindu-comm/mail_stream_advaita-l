Namaste Dennis ji.

In Advaita sampradAya, bhAvarUpatva of ajnAna is proved by Shruti pramANa
also.

You may like to refer to NAsadIya SUkta mantra 1 and 3.

Mantra 3 states - Tama Asset.

Mantra 1 states - na sat Aseet, na asat Aseet.

That is to say - at the start of the creation, there was neither sat nor
asat. But there was tamas. This shows that tamas is neither sat nor asat.
And it was.

So, a sat-asat-vilakshaNa tamas (ignorance) is accepted by Rigveda.

I don't know whether it serves your requirement of being explicit. If not,
then I would request you to explain mantra 1 and 3.

Regards.
Sudhanshu Shekhar.
