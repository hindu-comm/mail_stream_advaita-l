Namaste Chandramouli ji.

One can make his rules. But others are not bound to accept them na! Our
rules should be open for discussion. Just to remove objections, we cannot
change the rules of the game. Can we!

Simple question: Is avidyA avidyA-kalpita?

If yes, then how is it not AtmAshraya? If no, then avidyA no longer remains
adhyasta.

Regards.
Sudhanshu Shekhar.
