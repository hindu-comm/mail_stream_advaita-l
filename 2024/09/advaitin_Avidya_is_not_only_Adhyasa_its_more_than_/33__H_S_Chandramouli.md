Namaste.

What I meant was clear. An anAdi entity can be adhyasta without postulation
of a kAraNa. However all entities which have origination need a kAraNa.
Bhashya talks about origination of AkAsha, vAyu etc. They need a kAraNa as
they are sAdi. avidyA is stated to be their kAraNa. kAraNa as well as kArya
(effects) are however adhyAsa only. This does not in any way come in the
way of mUlAvidyA concept. However as I understand Sri SSS objection is that
avidyA is placed outside of adhyAsa in this concept and hence leads to
advaitahAni. This is not accepted by the other School. In fact this is what
I meant in the other post which I later withdrew. Now that a follower of
Sri SSS School has broached the topic, this aspect could be followed up if
felt appropriate. It is for others to debate as they deem fit. I will not
be participating.
