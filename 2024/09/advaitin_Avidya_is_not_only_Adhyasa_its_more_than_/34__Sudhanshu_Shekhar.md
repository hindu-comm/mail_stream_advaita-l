Namaste Raghav ji.

ajnAna-adhyAsa, which is kAraNa-adhyAsa, is anAdi and it ceases on
Brahma-jnAna. It is present in deep sleep.

All kArya-adhyAsa, starting from aham-adhyAsa, are sAdi and they cease in
deep sleep.

Regards.
