Kalyaanavrusti Mahaabhiyaana - Learning Resources

Pranaams,

Kalyaanavrusti Mahaabhiyaana is part of year long celebration of the
fiftieth year of sanyasa of His Holines Jagadguru Shankaracharya
Mahasannidhanam Sri Sri Bharati Theertha Mahaswamyji in the name of
"Suvarnabharati".

Given here are the resources made available for learning the three Stotras
choosen by Jagadguru Sannidhanam Sri Vidhu Shekhara Bharati Mahaswami for
this Mahaabhiyaana.

You can find
- Stotra Scripts of different languages in PDF format.
- Audio files of the stotras.
- Student-Teacher format videos for learning and praticse.
