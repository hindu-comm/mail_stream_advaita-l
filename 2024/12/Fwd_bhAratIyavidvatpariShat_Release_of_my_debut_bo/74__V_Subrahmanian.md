Namaste to all,

Gives me immense pleasure to let you all know that my debut
book 'Sankarayanam: The Journey of Sankara' has been released and is now
available on Amazon (https://amzn.in/d/9OI707k). Though we have many books
on the Acharya, this book is expected to be unique in its simple story
telling approach, treatment of various topics in a relatively unbiased way,
an FAQ section attempted to address some of the questions in the minds of
the contemporary generation, elaborate chapters on the theistic traditions
that existed during Acharya's time; his association with Yoga, Tantra; his
elaborate parampara; map of his Digvijaya travels; etc.

The book has been blessed with a Foreword by my Guru Gauri Mahulikar ji and
with testimonials from Swami Advayanand ji of Chinmaya Mission, Swami
Sakshatkritanandji of Arsha Vidya Gurukulam, Dr. Padma Subrahmanyam and Sri
Dushyanth Sridhar.

This is published under the INDICA label.

I would urge all great scholars here to read the book and give their
feedback/ review on the same. This will help me improve things for my next
venture :)

Please also share this in your networks so that this reaches more people.

Regards,
Subramanian Chidambaran
