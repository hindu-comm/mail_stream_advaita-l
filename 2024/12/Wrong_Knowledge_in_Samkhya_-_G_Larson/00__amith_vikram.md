*Wrong knowledge in the Sämkhya is neither a metaphysical entity nor an
ontological problem as it is for Sankara and the Vedänta. Wrong knowing is
simply the failure to make relevant distinctions whether on the level of
purusa and prakrti or on the level of the rope-snake.  *

Classical Sankhya - By G Larson
