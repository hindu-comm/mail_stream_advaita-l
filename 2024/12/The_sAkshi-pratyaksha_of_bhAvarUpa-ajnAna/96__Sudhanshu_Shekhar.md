Hari Om,

The issue of sAkshi-bhAsyatva of bhAvarUpa-ajnAna is one of the most
important concepts of advaita vedAnta. Without a correct understanding of
this, vedAnta cannot be correctly appreciated. The concepts are rooted in
our day-to-day experience of ignorance which have been analysed in
unimaginable depth by AchAryAs of advaita vedAnta.

I have made a study of this over a period of time and sought guidance from
scholars on several of the key issues. This has resulted in a rather
elaborate note spanning to more than eighty pages. I have put in effort to
systematically present the ideas. The note in PDF can be accessed from
https://sudhanshushekhar.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/the-sakshi-pratyaksha-of-bhavarupa-ajnana.pdf

The language in the note is quite Sanskrita-nishTha which may not be very
easy to appreciate at first glance. However, I am sure that with little
effort and by taking help from online LLMs in understanding the Sanskrit
words, one can understand the arguments easily. I am certain that this
effort will be extremely fruitful.

I am grateful to Dr Rahul Sarkhel from IIT Kanpur who has adorned the note
by writing a preface for it. He has been a constant support to me in
understanding the nuances of advaita vedAnta.

I am also grateful to Shri Lalitaalaalitah Swamiji and Shri Venkatraghavan
ji for their invaluable guidance from time-to-time on this topic.

I am also grateful to Dr Pushkar Deopujari ji whose workshop on Advaita
Siddhi organised by Ramkrishna Mission was extremely useful to me.

Having said this, any inaccuracies in the note are solely attributable to
me and not to the stalwarts mentioned above.

I request learned members and sincere seekers to go through the note
carefully. Any suggestions or discussion, as always, are most welcome.

Regards.
Sudhanshu Shekhar.
