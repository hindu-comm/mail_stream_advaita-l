Dear friends,
Bruhadaranyaka mantra 2-5-19 says:
ayamAtma brahma sarvAnuBUH ityanuSAsanam ||
TaittarIya mantra states: 
satyaM jnAnaM anantam brahma ||



And likethe space in the room, which cannot be seen but is always experienced, so ‘I’can never be found as an object of experience. Yet, it is always being experienced– that is, it is always experiencing itself.

This is the true and correct meaning of anuBUH /anuBava.

Please ponder over.



With respectful pranams,

Sreenivasa Murthy
