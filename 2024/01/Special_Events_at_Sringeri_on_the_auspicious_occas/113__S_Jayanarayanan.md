Special Events at Sringeri on the auspicious occasion of Prana Pratishtha at Ayodhya

SRINGERI
January 22, 2024 (Pushya Shukla Dwadashi)

As per the divine blessings and the instruction of Dakshinamnaya Sringeri Sri Sharada Peethadhishwara Jagadguru Shankaracharya Sri Sri Sri Bharati Tirtha Mahaswamiji
and Jagadguru Sri Sri Sri Vidhushekhara Bharati Mahaswamiji, the following events are scheduled to take place in Sringeri on the auspicious occasion of the Praana Pratishtha
of the Murti of Bhagavan Sri Ramachandra at Sri Rama Janmabhoomi Kshetram, Ayodhya.

Morning
7:30 AM : Mantra pushpa archana, special Naivedya and Mahamangala arati by the Jagadguru Mahaswamiji at Sri Kodanda Rama Swami temple in the Math premises.
8:00 AM : Jagadguru Mahaswamiji blesses the commencement of Akhanda Rama Nama Bhajana in front of Sri Kodanda Rama Swami temple
8:30 AM : Jagadguru Mahaswamiji blesses the commencement of the Parayana of Sampoorna Valmiki Ramayanam at Pravachana Mandira in the Math premises
9:30 AM : Jagadguru Mahaswamiji performs Punah-pratishtha Kumbhabhisheka at Sri Rama temple in Vidyaranyapura, Sringeri.
10:30 AM : Poornahuti of Sri Rama Taraka Homa in the divine presence of the Jagadguru Mahaswamiji at the temple of Sri Anjaneya, the guardian deity consecrated by Jagadguru Sri Adi Shankaracharya in the west of Sringeri
11:00 AM : Jagadguru Sri Mahasannidhanam and Jagadguru Sri Sannidhanam perform Mantrapushpa Archana and Mahamangala arati to Sri Ramachandra Murti at Gurunivas
12:00 Noon : Kumbhabhisheka Sabha Program and Anugraha Bhashanam of Jagadguru Mahaswamiji at Sri Rama temple, Vidyaranyapura.

Evening
5:30 PM : Chanting of Sri Rama Bhujanga Prayata Stotra followed by concert featuring the kritis on Bhagavan Sri Rama in the divine presence of the Jagadguru Mahaswamiji
6:30 PM : Jagadguru Mahaswamiji leads the Rathotsava of Bhagavan Sri Ramachandra in the Math premises
7:30 PM : Jagadguru Mahaswamiji commences the Deepotsava by lighting the ceremonial lamp and Mahamangala Arati is performed
8:00 PM : Completion of Akhanda Rama nama bhajana and Prasada distribution
