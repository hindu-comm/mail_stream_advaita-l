First Program of Baala Bharati (Commencing on Vyasa Puja day)

FOR REGISTRATION: https://forms.gle/96BsXabxFeHCgwm17

Baala Bharati, an initiative of the Dakshinamnaya Sri Sharada Peetham,
Sringeri, in association with Baala Veda, launches its 3-month long first
program for children, commencing on sacred Guru Purnima day, July 21, 2024.
This program is a part of the Suvarna Bharati Mahotsava, a year-long
celebration at the Peetham to mark the 50th year Sannyasa Sweekara of the
36th and reigning Acharya of the Peetham, Jagadguru Shankaracharya Sri Sri
Bharati Tirtha Mahaswamiji.

Through a blend of informative and fun activities, the program will:

1. Sensitise children to Bhakti & simple worship in our Dharma

2. Help reflect upon and cultivate core values essential for noble living

3. Familiarise with the life and teachings of Jagadguru Sri Adi
Shankaracharya, and the Sringeri Jagadgurus.

The program will be launched (virtually) by Jagadguru Sri Sri Vidhushekhara
Bharati Sannidhanam at the inaugural event at Sringeri Shankara Math in
Shankarapuram, Chamarajpet, Bengaluru, on Guru Purnima day. The Jagadguru
will also bless the program by initiating (virtually) the participating
children into a Japa, to be done by all participants through the duration
of the program. This will be followed by a Guru Puja by the children.

Participants who cannot attend the inauguration in person may view the
video streaming and participate online.

Program Duration: July 21 – Oct end, 2024

Eligibility: Open to all children aged 5 -14 years.

Program Delivery: The entire program of the “Baala Bharati” initiative will
be administered online. A limited number of Bangalore-based participants
can attend monthly meetings at the Sringeri Shankara Math in Shankarapuram,
Chamarajpet, Bengaluru (4 meetings in total, held over the weekend).

Specific details about the program will be provided upon registration using
the above link.
