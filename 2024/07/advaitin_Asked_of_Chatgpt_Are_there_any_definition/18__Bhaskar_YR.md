### Brhadaranyaka Upanishad Vartika

### Conclusion

Based on the examination of Suresvara's major works—the *Naiskarmya Siddhi*, the *Brhadaranyaka Upanishad Vartika*, and the *Taittiriya Upanishad Vartika*—it is evident that Suresvara consistently views avidya as a lack of knowledge rather than a positive, substantive entity. His writings emphasize that avidya is the ignorance of the true nature of the self, and it is dispelled through the attainment of self-knowledge.
