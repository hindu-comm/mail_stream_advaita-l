Dear Sudhanshuji,

Your various arguments of pot abhava - three gunas - abhava bhavarupa - pratiyogi are all based on the ontological considerations not epistemological. Avidya and adhyasa are epistemological errors not existential entities. The same logic simply does not apply.

The snake illusion does not require the snake to exist; it arises from misperception, not from a positive ontological snake. Similarly, ignorance need not have a positive existence to explain the concealment of Brahman.
