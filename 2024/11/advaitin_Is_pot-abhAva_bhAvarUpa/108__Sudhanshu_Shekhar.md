Namaste Michael ji.

Let us not digress. Let us leave everything aside and consider only the
following questions in light of anumAna presented by BhAshyakAra in
ghatA-bhAshya. Leave discussion of avidyA-adhyAsa etc.

Please try the following:

1. Is pot-abhAva bhAvarUpa?

2. Is pot-abhAva made of three guNAs namely sattva, rajas and tamas?

Regards.
Sudhanshu Shekhar.
