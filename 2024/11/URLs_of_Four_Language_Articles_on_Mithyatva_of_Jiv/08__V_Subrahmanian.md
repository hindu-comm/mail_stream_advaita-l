The Bhagavatam is replete with statements for:

1. Brahman itself appearing as the Jiva (Chit) and the Jagat (Achit)

2. That the jiva is unreal and so is the Jagat - Chit and Achit are both
unreal, leaving Brahman alone to be Real.

Here are articles on the above theme in Four Languages.  One can choose to
read the article in the language of his choice.
