The svarUpa of Ishvara is the tattvajnAni himself. Such an Ishvara is real. The jnAni can engage in a not real vyavahAra with his not real upAdhi-s based on a not real instruction from a real Ishvara with not real upAdhi-s.


  *   😊 😊 that summarizes the whole drama between socalled real Ishwara and real tattvajnAni-s…tattvajnAnis’ soliloquies considered as external IshwarAnujna.
