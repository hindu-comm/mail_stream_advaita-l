... just a clarification on samAsa used thus far vs the word quoted from
Prashnabhashya:
brahmaNi niShThA = brahmaniShThA, abidance in brahman (parabrahma in my
usage in all mails).
brahmaNi niSHThA yeShAM te = brahmaniShThAH, those (sages) who have
abidance in brahman (aparabrahma in Prashnabhashya).
