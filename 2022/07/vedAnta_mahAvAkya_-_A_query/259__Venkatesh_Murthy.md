Namaste

According to Vedanta Paribhasha there are Vyavaharika Tattvavedaka Pramanas
and Paramarthika Tattvavedaka Pramanas. The Mahavakyas and Abheda Sruti
vakyas like Sadeva Somya Idamagra are Paramarthika Tattvavedaka Pramanas.
All other Sruti Vakyas and Pramanas like Pratyaksha are Vyavaharika
Tattvavedka. Paramarthika Satya is higher than Vyavaharika Satya. It is not
possible to realize Brahman with the help of Vyavaharika Tattvavedaka Sruti
Vakyas. If you look at it this way there is a Taratamya in Sruti Vakyas
according to Advaitis.

Others will not make this and treat all Vakyas as equal.
