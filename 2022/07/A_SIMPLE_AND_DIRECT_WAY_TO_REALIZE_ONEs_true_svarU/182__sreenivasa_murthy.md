Mantra 4-4-23 of Bruhadaranyaks Upanishad reads thus :
AtmanyEvAtmAnaM paSyati sarvamAtmAnaM paSyati || Hesees the Self in his own self (body); he sees all as the Self.
Now, the question arises : how does he see Atman within himself?
Upanishads in a very very simple and direct way have revealed the methodology for cognizing one's true nature to become Atmavit by using the everyday experiences common to all human beings . Mantras 2-1--3 & 2-1-4 of Kathopanishad and mantra 2-4 of Kena Upanishad and Sri Shankara has written commentaries to these mantras which are profound. This is for Sure.
