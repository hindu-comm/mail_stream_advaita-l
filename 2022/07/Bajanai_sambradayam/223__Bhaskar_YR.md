Don't know anything about this bhajan group and claims.  But I too fond of bhajan-s, would like to listen to the bhajans of AOL, rAmakrishna mission etc.  nAma saMkeertana, japa, upAsana, dhyAna etc. definitely recommended in Advaita sAdhana also for chitta shuddhi so no qualms with that.  Chanting nAma and getting mOksha may not go well with Advaita sAdhana unless nAma saMkeertana gives us the paramArtha jnana to the non-dvija-s who do not have vedAdhikAra.  But it is also debatable that only sanyasi is eligible to attain mOksha.  All the 4 Ashramaites (brahmachAri, gruhastha, vAnaprasthi, sanyasi) and all 4 varNi-s (brAhmaNa, kshatriya, vaishya & shUdra)  are eligible for mOksha and for non-dvija-s there are specified sAdhana-s prescribed to attain paramArtha jnana / mOksha.  

Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
