Dear Colleagues,



We are pleased to announce that several new electronic texts are now
available in the Resource Library for Dharmaśāstra Studies
<https://sites.utexas.edu/sanskrit/resources/dharmasastra/> at UT-Austin.
The usual caveats about typos in these transcriptions apply. We want to
share these files as soon as they are complete, but we also try continually
to update any errors we find or that come to our attention.



   - Sarasvatīvilāsa by Pratāparudra
   <https://sites.utexas.edu/sanskrit/sarasvativilasa/>, digest on legal
   procedure
   - Pārāśara-mādhavīya
   <https://sites.utexas.edu/sanskrit/resources/dharmasastra/parasara-madhaviya/>,
   commentary on the Parāśara-smṛti
   - Divyatattva of Raghunandana Bhaṭṭācārya
   <https://sites.utexas.edu/sanskrit/resources/dharmasastra/divyatattva/>,
   digest on ordeals and oaths
   - Haradatta’s commentary (Mitākṣarā) on Gautama-dharmasūtra
   <https://sites.utexas.edu/sanskrit/resources/dharmasastra/haradatta-gautama/>
   - Haradatta’s commentary (Ujjvalā) on Āpastamba-dharmasūtra
   <https://sites.utexas.edu/sanskrit/resources/dharmasastra/haradatta-apastamba/>
   - Vyavahāranirṇaya of Varadarāja
   <https://sites.utexas.edu/sanskrit/vyavaharanirnaya/>, digest on legal
   procedure
   - Haradatta’s commentary (Anākulā) on Āpastamba-gṛhyasūtra
   <https://sites.utexas.edu/sanskrit/haradatta-apastamba-grhyasutra/>



Best,



Don Davis

Dept. of Asian Studies

University of Texas at Austin
