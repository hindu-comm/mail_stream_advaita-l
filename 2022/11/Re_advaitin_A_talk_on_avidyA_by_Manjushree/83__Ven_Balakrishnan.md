The point is any conceptual framework one has, is in the mind, which is part of the ignorance.  So who is to say if perception still remains when one is a jnani?  It is like eka jiva vada  And the fact is there are different passages in Sankara’s bhasya, which could support different interpretations. 

The one point that is clear is there is a severe attenuation of the ego, and disidentification with the body mind.
