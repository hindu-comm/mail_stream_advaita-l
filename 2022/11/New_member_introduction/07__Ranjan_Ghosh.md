Ashish Payasi ji,
 Iam replying to you as I live close to Greater Noida and
gone through similar experience in life. Very happy to know about your
growing interest in Advaita philosophy. One of the finest place to interact
with monks is Advait Ashram Mayawati in Champawat district Uttarakhand.
Actually you can participate in a retreat named Advaitamritam from 14-20
Nov 2022. In my view, this is a golden opportunity to exercise
Shravan-Manan-Nididhyasan in a serene environment in Himalayas. You can
visit the site https://advaitaashrama.org/ for details.
