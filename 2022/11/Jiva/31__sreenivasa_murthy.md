Dear friends,
Sri Shankara says :
(1) tasmAtparabrahmavyatirEkENa saMsArInAma 

nAnyadvastvantaramasti||

[Commentary To mantra Bruhadaranyaka; Mantra 2-1-20]



(2) kiMtunaivAbrahma avidyAkartA cEtanO 

BrAnta anya iShyatE||

[Commentary To mantra Bruhadaranyaka; Mantra 1-4-10]



(3) brahmAvyatiriktASca 

sarvE vij~JAnAtmanaH||

[Commentary To mantra TaittarIya upanishad; ]



(4) na hi nityamuktasvarUpAtsarvajnAt 

ISvarAt anyaH cEtanO DhatuH dvitIyaH 

vEdAntArthanirUpaNAyAm||

[Commentary To brahmasutra; 2-3-20]

What do the above BAshyavAkyas point out to?

Since no second conscious principle otherthan Atman / Brahman does exist where is the question of EkajIva ornAnAjIva? 
With respectful namaskars,Sreenivasa Murthy
