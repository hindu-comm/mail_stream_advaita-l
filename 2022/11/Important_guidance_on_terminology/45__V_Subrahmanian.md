> I require guidance on these following terms and the differences between
> them from our important pramanas (Sruthi and Bhashya preferably).
>
> 1. Bhu Loka
> 2. Bhu Mandala
>

Both mean the same - the entire world, just one of the 14 lokas in the
Brahmanda. For instance Rama, even though Ayodhyadhipati, is also regarded
as samasta bhUmandala adhipati.


> 3. Saptha Mandala  - There is a Sapta-rishi-mandala.  A constellation.
>



> 4. Jagath
> 5. Prapancham
>

Jagat and Prapancha both mean - the world. The entire created universe.

We do not need pramana for these usages.
