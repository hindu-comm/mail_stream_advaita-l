Anointing of Shankaracharya of Jyotishpeeth and Dwarkapeeth: Shankaracharya of Sringeripeeth presented Silver Kamandal to Sadanand and Swami Avimukteshwaranand

SEPTEMBER 28, 2022

STATES: UTTAR PRADESH


After the Shankaracharya of Dwarkapeeth, Swaroopanand Saraswati, his disciples Avimukteshwarananda and Sadananda Saraswati have been made his successors.
Both the successors were given the seat of Jyotish Peeth and Dwarkapeeth. Swaroopanand Saraswati had the responsibility of both the Peeths. This morning both
the successors were consecrated amidst Vedic chants at the temple of Goddess Shardamba, the presiding deity of Sringeri Peeth in Karnataka. During this,
both the monasteries resolved to work unitedly.

Jagadguru Shankaracharya Sri Bharathi Tirtha Mahaswami of Dakshinamnaya Sringeripeeth gave the responsibility of the Peeth to both the Shankaracharyas by
Vedic rituals. He said that now Abhishek programs for both the new Shankaracharyas will be organized at many places across the country.

The hand placed on the head of the Jagadgurus

Shankaracharya Sri Bharathi Teertha Mahaswami placed his hand on the heads of both the Jagadgurus before performing the abhishek. Announced in Sanskrit.
“I am consecrating Swami Avimukteshwarananda Saraswati, the successor disciple of Brahmalin Dwipeethadhishwaratwar Jagadguru Shankaracharya Maharaj,
at Jyotishpeeth and Swami Sadananda Saraswati at Paschimmannaya Dwarkapeeth.” Shankaracharya also presented sweet silver kamandalu to both the newly appointed
Shankaracharyas. Both expressed a feeling of gracious respect towards him. Reaffirmed its commitment to the unity of the three monasteries with the
Sringeripeeth.

After 20 days there will be other legislation on Badarikashram, Himalayas

After the consecration, Mahaswami ji also made an announcement. Said, “After 20 days, in the auspicious time, his successor Swami Vidhushekhar Bharati Maharaj
will go to Dwarkapeeth and Jyotrimath Badarikashram, Himalayas. After being installed as Jagadguru, he will fulfill all the religious rituals that are
performed.”

In 2007, Swaroopanand had announced to make both the successors.

In the year 2007 itself, the Chatushpeeth conference organized by Vedanta Bharati Sanstha of Bangalore was held. Brahmalin Jagadguru Shankaracharya Swaroopanand Maharaj
presented both of them as his successors to Sringeripeethadhishvara Maharaj. At the same time, Brahmalin Shankaracharya Maharaj had also told that after I become Brahmalin,
you will get all the necessary religious works done for the succession of these two. Keeping the remembrance and respect of the same word, Shri Bharati Tirtha Mahaswami
got this Abhishek done.

His presence in the monastery

During the consecration, Brahmachari Dhyananand, Brahmachari Uddhav Swaroop, Acharya P. Rajendra Dwivedi, Acharya Pt. Parameshwar Dutt Shukla of Bharat Dharma Mahamandal,
Acharya P. Kamalakant Tripathi of Kashi Vidvat Parishad, Pt. Prakash Upadhyay from Chhattisgarh, Anand Upadhyay etc. Saints-Mahatmas were present in the Math.
