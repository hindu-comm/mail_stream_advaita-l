Interestingly dvaitins too do accept jeeva is amara, aja, shuddha (some mukti yOgya jeeva-s) etc.  But jeeva and jagat have the dependent existence and Ishwara would control it.  And the jeeva's realization that he is amruta/aja etc.  that-is-not the final realization, after realizing his 'svarUpa',  jeeva has to do sAdhana to get the mukti like saarupya, saalokya, saayujya etc.  So, definitions something relating to jeeva's immortality etc. do not bother them much as this is the subsidiary  realization before approaching the final mukti.  

Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
Bhaskar YR
