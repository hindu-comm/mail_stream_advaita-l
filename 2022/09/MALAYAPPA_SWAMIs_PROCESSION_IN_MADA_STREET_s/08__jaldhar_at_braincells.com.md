And what of the worshippers?  Let's face it, most of them are also there 
for money, money, money or atleast some kind of personal advantage.

Bhagavan appears in the form suited to the devotee.

A tamasik bhakta prays because he is afraid he will some misfortune or 
punishment in hell etc. if he does not.

A Rajasik bhakta prays because he thinks he will gain something from it, 
riches, prestige and acclaim from society etc.

A Sattvik bhakta prays because he loves God.  But even he might be 
motivated by the good feelings this causes rather than pure selflessness.

This is why previously I pushed back against the idea that Bhakti is an 
easy path.  It also has treacherous pitfalls for the unprepared.  But for 
those rare ones who truly love Bhagavan without ego, and whose minds are 
fixed entirly on Bhagavan alone, Bhagavan will always put such VIPs 
(Vishnu Intoxicated Persons) at the head of the line for his darshan.
