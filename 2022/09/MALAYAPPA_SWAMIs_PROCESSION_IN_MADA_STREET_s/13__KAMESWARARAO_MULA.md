Dear Members,              It is often said that 'Kalau Venkata Nayaka', which simple means There is no other wish fulfilling God other than Lord Venkateswara in the Kali Yuga. He is dwelling on Seshachala in the Ananda Nilayam & Yashobhuta dhari is making the peope Happy by fulfilling the manokamana's of the various types of disciples. You can see happiness every where on the hill if you limit yourself with your purpose of visit / Job.
If you stats arguing with any policies/procedures, you ends up with argument only. Logical solutions can be found by approaching the TTD board electronically. TTD always welcomes any complaints/suggestions/need of the procedure changes online in their website.
There is worth mentioning of Nada Neerajana Mandapam opposite to temple in which evening discourses by temple pandits, classical music programmes of various artist in praise of the God, Annamayya Kirtana competitions goes live in TTD channel through out all days.
I can be of any help electronically for anybody's visit to Tirumala Hills & can guide how to book online seva tickets with your aadhar card/photo based logins into their website.
I recommend anyone to visit in groups/ with full family members & the purpose of the visit is fulfilled for which Lord Venkateswara's blessings are assured.
One should also pay attention to sudden Rain on the Hill during Rainy season (July-october).
