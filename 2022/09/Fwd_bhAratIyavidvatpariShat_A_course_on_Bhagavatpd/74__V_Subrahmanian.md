A course on Bhagavatpāda's *Śivānandalaharī* is offered by Prof.K
Ramasubramanian <https://www.kramasubramanian.com/> (IIT Bombay) on

participants. Recordings of the classes will later be made available
on the Advaita

Earlier participants of the Vedantamuktavali course need not register to
get the course information.* New learners, who would like to join the
course, are welcome to register at: https://www.kramasubramanian.com/live
<https://www.kramasubramanian.com/live>*

To the learners who register for the course (and to Vedantamuktavali course
participants), the Zoom link and other course details will be shared via
mail on 28th September. There are no course fees involved. Out of reverence
to our Ācārya and to our tradition, participants are requested to keep the
cameras open and participate in the sessions.
