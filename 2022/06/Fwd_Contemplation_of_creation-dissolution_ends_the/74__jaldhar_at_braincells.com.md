> You write : " The Supreme Lord's teaching is that when we contemplate 
> upon the concept of creation from the One and its involution into that 
> One, we transcend the delusion of duality."How can by contemplating a

When one is afflicted by delusion, one has no choice but to deal with 
concepts.

The mistake I see would-be Advaitins make over and over again is to simply 
assume the conclusions of Vedanta without understanding how we got there. 
Those types tend to have very poor understanding and behavior as a result 
and I doubt many will succeed in the goal of liberation from samsara.

In other words, as our maths teachers used to tell us, don't just give an 
answer, show your work!
