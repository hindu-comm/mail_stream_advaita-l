 In the bagavtham there is a story of shri krishna taking away rukmini devi.
Rukmini
Devi writes a letter to shri krishna asking him to take her away. But she
is a bit worried if shri krishna might come or not. But the lord indeed
comes to rescue her. In the same way as rukmini devi had doubts, a baktha
too has doubts if the lord is listening to his prayers or if he has
accepted the devotee. This story reminds us that when there is true bakthi
and the object of desire is the lord, the lord shall always bring the
baktha to him. Such is the lord's karuna and daya. Just a few thoughts i
had as tomorrow is the auspicious day of ekadashi.
