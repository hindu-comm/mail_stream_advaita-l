The Smaarta Advaita sampradaya has produced numerous stalwarts since ages.
One such Mahatman was Shri Karapatri ji Maharaj ( Shri Hariharaananda
Saraswati). Swamiji was a great Shaastra-vidvaan, a great Shri
Vidyopaasaka, a strict adherent and proponent of Vaidika Varnaashrama
dharma. Maharaj ji’s efforts in ensuring the welfare of Gau-vamsha can
never be forgotten by us. It is a matter of delight to all astikaas that
Maharaj ji’s contributions have been formally recognized by the Government
of India and a commemorative postage stamp has been issued with Swamiji’s
image. 🙏🏻🙏🏻🙏🏻
