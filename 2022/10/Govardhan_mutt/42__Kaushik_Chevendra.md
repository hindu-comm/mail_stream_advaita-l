Namaste sir.
I understand what you are saying. There is no doubt that we are indebt to
swami vivekananda who has bought unparalleled fame to Hinduism. But we must
never forget lord Krishnas words in gita. The shastras alone have to be
taken as a pramana to decide what is right or wrong. If we ignore the
injunctions of shastra and act as per our own logic, cannot expect to grow
spiritually. Hence our shistas always talk in accordance to shastras only.
