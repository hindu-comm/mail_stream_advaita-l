You are on the right track. The only takeaway from modern Physics is the
importance of the observer. Without the observer there is no reality. The
reality of the universe depends on the observer.

But Science stops at the Observer and that is where Advaita begins. It
demands minimum qualifications like the four fold qualifications for those
who want to study Vedanta.

Let me be blunt and say for a person lacking in these qualifications the
Universe is Real. If he starts treating it as Unreal he is doomed to
Spiritual Death.
