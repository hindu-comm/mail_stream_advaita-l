As far as Advaita Sidhanta is concerned, the issue is who is Real,
**Observer** or **Observed**. Science, including the latest scientific
discovery referred to relates to the **Observed** with reference to the
**Observer**. But in that discovery, how **Real** is the **Observer** with
reference to whom **Reality** of the **Observed** is investigated. There is
hardly any investigation in that respect as I understand.
