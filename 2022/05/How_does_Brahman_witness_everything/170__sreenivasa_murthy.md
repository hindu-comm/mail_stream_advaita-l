 Dear Sri Kameshwararao,
Sakshi cannot be sakshyam. But Sakshyam is Sakshi only.There are no two things like sakshi and sakshi accordingto Upanishads.Sri Shankara writes in his commentary to mantra 2-1-19 
of Bruhadaranyaka Upanishad as follows: AtmavyatirEkENa vastvantarABAvAt ||Kathopanishad declares : nEha nAnAsti kiMcana ||

Do you know this fact?Please realize / cognize this fact.For this purpose the totality of LIFE consisting of waking, dream and deep sleephas to be examined.Are you familiar with these two mantras from Chandogya Upanishad Viz. ahamEvadagM sarvam || 7-25-1 ,AtmaivEdagM sarvam || 7-25-2and Atmata EvEdagM sarvam || 7-26-1.
I request you kindly to have a good and solid foundation of Upanishadicteachings.With respectful namaskars,Sreenivasa Murthy.
