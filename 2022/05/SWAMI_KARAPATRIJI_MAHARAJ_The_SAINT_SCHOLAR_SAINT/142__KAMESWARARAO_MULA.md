Dear Members,              Some Chronology of Event in Life of Swami Karpatriji:
12. In the Year 1940 C.E (Vikramabda 1997), He established Dharma sangha. He performed Abhisheka to his Diksha guru Shree Swami Brahmmananda Saraswati (Kasi Shankaracharya) at the Uttaramnaya Jyothirmathi peetham, followed by his book on "Samanvay Samrajya Sanrakshan.
13. In the same year he had intense discussion on Shasta's with Mahamahopadhyay Shree Padit Haihar Kripalu.
14. He wrote a book on Bhagawattattva in the year 1940 while continuing his travel.
15. In 1942, He held a religious conference at Prayagraj during 13-14-15 days of Dark moon on behalf of Dharmasangha. He installed people todo various jobs out of his inner core for the welfare of Bharatavasrha. He retuned back to varanasi.
16. He travelled north western states of india like Punjab, Rajasthan, UP to install Dharmic Anusthan among various people before returning to temple town varanasi.
17. During 1940-41, He started to Gangasagar from varansi. At kolkatta, he held conference on Varnashrama Swarajya Sangha. He performed Shatachandi anushtan according to resoution of dharmasangha. At Bhagalpur Vaidyanadh dham, he established a branch of Dharmasangha.
