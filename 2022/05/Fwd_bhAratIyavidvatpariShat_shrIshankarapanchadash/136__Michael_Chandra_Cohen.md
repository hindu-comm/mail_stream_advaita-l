Sri V. Subramanian and all,

S.S. Raghavachar is a Ramanuja guy. His preface to Sri Bhasya announces
that the Sankara Sutra Bhasya idea of nirguna brahman as the ultimate
teaching of Vedanta is wrong as, "it has been established conclusively (in
this text) that the supposedly higher teaching attributed to the text as
its authentic purport is entirely without evidence"
https://archive.org/details/sribhashyaonthephilosophyofthebrahmasutrassraghavachar1986ocr/page/n11/mode/2up

> Namaste!  I would like you to listen to this from Minute 22 on, where the
> mahaswami is asserting that Sri Sankara's conception of bhakti is not the
> same as that of dvaitins and therein lies a lot of confusion. "
>
> In Viveka chudamani, "sva-svarupanusandhanam bhaktirityabhidheeyate" is
> mentioned as a quick definition of bhakti by Shankaracharya himself.
> However, in the Gita bhashya of Shankaracharya, the explanation of bhakti
> is very unique and only a focused study of it with an open mind will give
> you an interesting outlook. One would wonder, how the term sva-svarupa
> anusandhanam can be justified with a clear bheda-view of bhakti for the
> initial as well as a mature bhakti yogi.  Prof SS Raghavachar has done a
> great job in his paper. I request scholars in this forum to critique this
> paper. I am sure this will be of great value to everyone, irrespective of
> their firm conviction. After reading this paper carefully, I am sure that
> you will gain a different perspective on this topic.
