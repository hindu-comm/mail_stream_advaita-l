It is well known that the name *adhyasa bhashya* has not been mentioned by
Shankara. It is now learnt that this name has been first used in the
*Bhamati* in about 6 places. In the Panchapadika this word does not exist.
Nor does Sureshvaracharya mentions this in the Taittiriyaka bhashya
vartika. We do not know if this name exist in the Brihadaranyaka bhashya
vartika or in the  Naishkarmya  siddhi.
