Indica Moksha <https://www.indicamoksha.com/> (Advaita Academy) invites
entries for an essay competition as part of the Global Festival of Oneness
2022 celebrating the life and works of Jagadguru Adi Shankaracharya.

Adi Shankaracharya is the greatest exponent of Advaita Vedanta, the school
of thought that highlights the oneness of individual and Brahman.

His major works are the commentaries on Bhagavad Gita, major Upanishads and
Brahma Sutra, preliminary Vedanta texts such as Upadesha Sahasri,
Vivekachudamani, Atmabodha and Aparokshanubhuti. He has also composed
hundreds of devotional hymns such as Soundaryalahiri and Subramanya
Bhujanga.

Adi Shankaracharya’s influence has not only been on Advaita Vedanta but on
entire Hinduism. He established four Mathas in four parts of India to
promote the practice of Sanatana Dharma.

Details on the Essay Competition are as follows:

 Topic:

Is Adi Shankara a role model for modern Hindus?

Adi Shankara – A monk who travelled to teach

Write about any three works of Adi Shankara and how they have inspired you

Adi Shankara in my daily life.

Medium of Essay: English

Words:  1000-1500

Age Eligibility: 15-30 years

Last Date for Submission: May 25, 2022

Three winners will be picked at the end of the festival and their works
will be published in Indica Today. They will also be given a cash prize of
Rs. 2000/-

Please choose any ONE of the listed topics and submit your essays to
nithin at advaita-academy.org

Looking forward to hearing from you all.
