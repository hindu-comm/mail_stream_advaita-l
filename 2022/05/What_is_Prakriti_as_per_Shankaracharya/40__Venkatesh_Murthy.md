> Could you please explain this paramarthika reality.
>
> Is this paramarthika reality a higher samadhi like experience?

It is not Samadhi because Samadhi is experienced in the  mind. Mind is also
Maayaa.
The highest Reality is what the enlightened person becomes. He does not
feel I am experiencing Brahman. He feels I am Brahman without Duality.
