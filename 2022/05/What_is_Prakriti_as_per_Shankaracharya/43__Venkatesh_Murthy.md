Only a living person can speak. We have to assume the enlightened person
speaking is a Jeevan Mukta with Avidyalesha.

Without Avidyalesha he cannot do anything like speaking, walking and so
on.

Because he has Avidyalesha he can see Duality and be Brahman at the
same time. This is nicely explained in Panchadashi.

It is like seeing some object like a tree and its reflection in a lake at
the same time. It is possible.

because it is implying " I "  and  "Brahman "  and  "" feeling "".
