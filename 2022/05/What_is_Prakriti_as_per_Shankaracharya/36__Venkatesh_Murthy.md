> Namaste Venkatesh ji.
>
> Will it be correct to say that prakriti or maya is just a manifestation
> (vyakta state) of the impersonal formless Brahman?
>

Yes for the ignorant person and inquiring person because Brahman is the
Creator. No for the enlightened person. For him there is no Creator
Creation.
