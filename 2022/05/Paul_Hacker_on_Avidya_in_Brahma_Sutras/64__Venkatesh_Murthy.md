Kindly read your own reply above. If Mula Avidya is said to be
Vyavaharikasatta or relatively real then where is the problem? Nobody said
Mula Avidya is Paramarthikasatta. The tradition says it is Bhava Rupa
because you cannot say the world has evolved from a total Abhava. Bhava
Rupa means that is not Abhava only. It does not mean it is Absolutely real
like Brahman. The opponents of Mula Avidya principle shout  and argue as if
the traditionalists said it is  Paramarthika. Nobody said that.
