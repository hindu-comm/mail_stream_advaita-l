The Centre for Brahmavidya is pleased to announce that the audio recordings
of the Prasthana-traya Bhashya classes (in Tamil) of the reputed and
revered scholar, Dr Mani Dravid Sastri, have been fully uploaded to the
website www.centreforbrahmavidya and that those interested can click here
(and go to 'Audio') to listen to the recordings made available therein.

Earlier, Dr Mani Dravid Sastri’s bhashya classes on a few Upanishads and
the Brahmasutras had been uploaded to the site. Now, the bhashya classes on
the remaining Upanishads (forming part of the Ten major Upanishads) and the
Bhagavadgita have also been uploaded, thus covering the complete
Prasthana-traya.

The classes have been nicely indexed and are easy to navigate.

Please share this announcement with relevant groups/forums.
