Krishna says Dharma aviruddhah Kamo Asmi. Kama within the boundaries of
Dharma is allowed. If a married person has no children he may have desire
and may go for Putra Kameshti or some rituals for getting a child because
that is not against Dharma. Aputrasya Gatir Nasti.
