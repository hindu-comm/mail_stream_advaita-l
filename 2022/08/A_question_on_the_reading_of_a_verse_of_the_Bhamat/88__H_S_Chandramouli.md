The question was not clear to me. However I just want to point out that all
texts of VP that I have come across, namely edited by Swami Madhavananda,
Prof Suryanarayana Shastri, Sri Anantakrishna Shastri  have the same version

<<  विद्याकर्मस्वनुष्ठानतोषितेश्वरचोदितम् ।
अधिकारं समाप्यैते प्रतिशन्ति परं पदम् ॥  >>.
