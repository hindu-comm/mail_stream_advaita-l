I am happy to share that my Tamil Translation of Hathayogapradeepika with
detailed notes from Jyotsna commentary has been published by Indic Academy.
This work has been undertaken after 126 years (earlier translation done in
the year 1896 by Yogi Sri Kuppuswami Raju of Thanjavur).

The features and details about the work are as follows  -

Hathayoga-Pradeepika: Written in the fifteenth century by Yogi Svatmarama,
this book serves as a guide to current day yoga practices. 15 Asanas, 6
Kriyas 8 Pranayamas, 10 Mudras, and Meditative practices such as
Nadanusandhana are explained in this book.

After 126 years, this book has now been translated by Dr M Jayaraman for
present day Tamil readers. No. pages 385, 19 illustrations.

Details - https://indicabooks.org/books/hatha-yoga-pradipika/
Amazon Link - https://www.amazon.in/dp/B0B7MHN75S
