Today many Muslim friends celebrates Muharram. Muharram ceremonies in South Asia in general and India in particular have attracted deep reverence and devotion from Muslims and non-Muslims alike.
Varanasi, for instance, has a tradition of commemorating Muharram where some Hindu families participate in the procession of Taziya Sharif. This also happens in Lucknow, Prayagraj, Kanpur and many other cities. The observance of Muharram ceremonies has introduced Islam as the harbinger for interfaith understanding.
