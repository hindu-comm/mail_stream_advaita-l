Hello sir,

Does it really matter whether Sankara was Shaivite or Vaishnavite. He is advaitin. He didn't differentiate any form of Supreme reality and wrote stotras on all forms, didn't he.


Also as T.U. says.

Shivaaya Vishnu Roopaaya, Shiva Roopaaya Vishanave |
Shivasya Hrudayam Vishnur, Vishnuscha Hrudayam Shivaha ||
Yatha Shivamayo Vishnuhu, Yevam Vishnu Mayaha Shivaha |

Yathaantharam Na Paschyaami, Thatha Me Swasthi Ra Yushi ||


Best Regards/  VTCS
