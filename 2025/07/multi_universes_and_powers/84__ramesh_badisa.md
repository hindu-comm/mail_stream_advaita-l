Namaskaram to all.As per Upanishads (e.g: Maha NarayanaUpanishad), Puranams (e.g: Brahmanda puranam) and modern astronomy, there are manyUniverses in the vast expansion of space. As per our scriptures quoted above, each Universe has a Brahma, a Vishnu and a Maheswara. I would like to know from fellow advaitins if Omnipresent, Omnipotent, andOmniscience of Trinity are limited to their respective Universes only. Grateful if you share your thoughts on this.Namaskaram.ramesh
