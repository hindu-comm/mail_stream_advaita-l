Namaste.
In the Geeta Bagavan tells Arjuna that with one portion of himself he has
pervaded the entire visible universe. Acharya quotes the rig Veda to
validate this statement. So it’s very clear that “vishnu” is not limited to
this Brahmananda or another.
Think of it this way, Parameshwara who is manifest as the entire universe
can manifest himself as shiva/vishnu in each Brahmananda for sadaka/devata
anugraham. In Bagavatham there a story where multiple Brahmas come for each
Brahmananda to worship Krishna.

Narayana
