Namaste everyone,

Probably in 1987 or so, it was three of us (Mani, Vidyashankar, and I) on a
conference bridge paying at least $3 per minute for an hour or more!. We
were discussing :

"yatra hi dvaitam iva bhavati, tad itara itaram pasyati."

‘You will see another only when there is duality’.

*Immediately after this discussion,  Vidyasankar started the Advaita
discussion Group, and Mani started Bhakti-list.*

Welcome, Vidya Sankar!




*Krishna Kashyap*
