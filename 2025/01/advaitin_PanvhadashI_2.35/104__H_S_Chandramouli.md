Namaste Bhaskar Ji,

For Sri Bhagavatpada, Brahman is the ever **existing** adhishthAna. Subject
to this only, any **other** adhishthAna may or may not be essential. That
is exactly what the verse under discussion states. Are you saying Sri
Bhagavatpada does not insist on even Brahman being necessary as adhishthAna
for bhrAnti?
