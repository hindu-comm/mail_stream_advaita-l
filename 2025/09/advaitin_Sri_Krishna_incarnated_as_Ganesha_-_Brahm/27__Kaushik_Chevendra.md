Adding to it Bagavan himself says in Geeta -
bahūnāṁ janmanām ante jñānavān māṁ prapadyate
vāsudevaḥ sarvam iti sa mahātmā su-durlabhaḥ
After many births, the wise one surrenders unto Me, realizing: *Vasudeva is
everything*. Such a great soul (mahatma) is very rare.”


Namo Narayana
