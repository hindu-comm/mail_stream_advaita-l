Thank you Subbu ji,
A very interesting article. They laugh best who laugh last, it is said, but
we may add, with or without reason. They have the privilege of laughing at
the earlier doctrines. People do so many things to satisfy their egos and
the egos of innocent followers for the fault of being born in a tradition.

sitting on the hilltop and watching the tumult below (as Vidyaranya says),
we can smile at the audacity of such writings. Anyway, the article is worth
preserving.
Aravinda rao.
