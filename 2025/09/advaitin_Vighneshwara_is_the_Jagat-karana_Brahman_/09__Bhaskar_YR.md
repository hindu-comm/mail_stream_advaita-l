A jnani can continue in believing in jagat karana etc. he just realizes that Brahman is not _only_ the jagat karana, that Brahman is so much _more_than jagat karan.

>  Yes, that is why shruti says for the jagat brahman is ekaM eva abhinna nimittOpadAna kAraNa but something beyond that also.  atyatishTa dashAgulaM says veda.  Yes brahman is in its very nature is without prANa, manas he is pure 'aprANohyamaNAH shubhraH'  but from him only the prANa, manas and all Indriya-s have been originated etasmAtjjaayate prANaH manassarvendriyAni.  The bhAshyakAra also confirms this in katha shruti bhAshya by clarifying : the brahman is completely and totally nirguNa, nirvishesha, niravayava. However, It DOES exist because It is known to be the cause of the Jagat.
