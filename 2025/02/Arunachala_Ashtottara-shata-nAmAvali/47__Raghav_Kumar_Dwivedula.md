, I had a doubt about the following names, viz.,

1.   ॐ आहुवैश्वर्यदाताय नमः
आहू + ऐश्वर्यदातः
Call/Invoke + (gave aishvarya)
Salutations to the One who gave aishvarya to the one who calls or invokes
Him.

Here my first impression was, even if the word AhU is taken in the
bahuvRIhi sense of the one who invokes Shiva, the word   आहुवैश्वर्यदात्रे
नमः  is better although दातः ( given) appears contrived , but is
permissible, it appears.


2.   ॐ व्यतस्त-नृत्याय नमः
Salutations to The one who displayed the cosmic dance
Is it ॐ त्यातस्तनृत्याय नमः rather than व्यवस्तनृत्याय नमः ?
From त्यातनोति (display or produce)

( ॐ  वेदमूर्तये नमः ? )
