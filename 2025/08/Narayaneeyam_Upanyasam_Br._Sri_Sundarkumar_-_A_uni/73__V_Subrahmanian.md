bhakti rasika, shaastra rasika, sangeetha rasika and so on.  His discourses
abound in many such facets.  He enriches them with countless samples from
Tamil, Sanskrit, Telugu, Marathi, at times even English literature. One
gets to benefit immensely by listening to him. Here is Part 2 of the
Narayaneeyam.  The remaining ones can be accessed from YouTube.
