Namaste Subbuji
Particularly interesting was the proposed lecture titled “Krishna, the
master strategist” by Sri Vishwanath Ji.

Sri Krishna’s astounding role in refashioning the whole society of 3000 BCE
India, such as how he validated and blessed the Rajasuya Yagya of
Yudhishthira, crowing him as the Chakravarty of Bharata (the Indian
subcontinent, comprising all areas from Afghanistan to Bangladesh (And
possibly control extending up to Bali in South East Asia ) points to his
role in working for the political unification of BharataVarsha. And Sri
Krishna Bhagavan used optimal but unapologetic force in achieving that
unification end - which was clearly enunciated by him as a desirable goal
for the protection of Dharma.

Om
Raghav
