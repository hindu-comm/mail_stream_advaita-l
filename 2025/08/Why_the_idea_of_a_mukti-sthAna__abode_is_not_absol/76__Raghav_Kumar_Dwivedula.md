Namaste Subbu ji
Thank you for the article. Is there any yukti directly given in any verse
that any concept of “Nitya vaikunṭha loka” with the word loka as a special
divine *eternal* place etc., is logically impossible because “sAvayavatvAt
anityatvaM” or “dRshyatvAt anityatvaM” etc. Anything that is seen of has
parts is non-eternal. While yukti stands on its own legs, still
sometimes such yuktis are given in Shruti or smRti giving them a certain
finality.
