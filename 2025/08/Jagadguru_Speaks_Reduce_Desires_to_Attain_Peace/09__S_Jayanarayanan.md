Man must conduct his life with due consideration of certain
aspects such as the limitless objects perceived in this illusory
world and the waywardness of his own mind, to run after them.

Multifarious desires crop up in man's mind from time to time.
Even a little introspection into such desires will reduce the
intensity of their pull. However, nurturing excessive desires
will only destroy one's peace of mind, as there is no limit to
one's desires. The realisation of one desire will immediately
spawn more desires which will entangle man in an endless
chase and eventually destroy his life. A mind that is not content
will not experience any peace or calm.

In this context, the story of the hen laying golden eggs must
be familiar to most. This is also what our forefathers have
instructed.

स तु भवति दरिद्रो यस्य तृष्णा विशाला |
मनसि च परितुष्टे कोऽर्थवान्‌ को दरिद्रः ||

He with no control over his desires is truly poor and helpless.
An oft-quoted proverb says, “The mind which considers what it
has as enough will find true wealth.” Only a person who has a
sense of contentment and sufficiency can be considered truly
prosperous. This is because contentment and peace of mind are
man's greatest treasures.

The crux of the matter is that desires have no limit and
nurturing them will rob man of all peace.

Our blessings for everyone to understand this well and live
contentedly with what comes their way.
