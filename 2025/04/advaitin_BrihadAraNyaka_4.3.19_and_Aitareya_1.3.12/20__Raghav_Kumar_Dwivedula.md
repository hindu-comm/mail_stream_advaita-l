Namaste Sudhansuji
That pratyabhijnA is bhrAnti is beautifully expressed in DSV explained in
the verse quoted by you from AS.

At the same time, is there not a distinction in DSV between some of the
entities about which we could assert their very experience is their
creation as opposed to 'anAdi' entities within DSV?

In dream, I would understand the experience of time (or any other putative
'anAdi' entity) too as arising or created afresh with each experience of
that dream.

 If I grant anything we experience in dream (such as time etc) an anAdi
status, would that not lead to acceptance of pratyabhijnA leading to
compromise of DSV?

Is there not the mention of anAdi entities within DSV  as per AS?

Om
Raghav
