Dear Sudhanshu Ji,

In this Gita Bhashyam Shankara gives the hetu jneyatvaat to determine
something as kshetram, aka prakruti. This is not different from the hetu
drishyatvaat. Anything that is object to the saakshi is kshetram, the whole
of which has been determined in the last verse of this chapter to be
mithya, abhavagamanam.

This is a very eminent DSV statement from the Bhashyakaara.

Regards
subbu
