>> Atma Chaitanya/Musta Ram/Ira Shepetin & Michael Chandra
>> Manana & Meditation
>> Each morning we will first sit quietly and calm the mind in preparation
>> for guided reflections that unfold the teachings of the classical texts and
>> of Shankara, the most important Acharya of the nondual tradition.
>> Meditation will be followed by chanting and then group discussions among
>> participants. Questions and answers will follow as required.
>> Fundamental wisdom teachings of Vedanta
>> This is the centrepiece of the intensive course. Our afternoons will be
>> filled with lectures highlighting the core principles of the classical
>> texts: The 10 Upanishads, the Bhagavad Gita and the Brahma Sutras, as well
>> as the precepts taught by Shankaracharya and others familiar with this
>> original methodology. The discussions will revolve around these topics,
>> among others:
>>
>> 1. What is the true nature of the self?
>> 2. What exactly is the ignorance that causes all of life's problems?
>> 3. What is the difference between doing something and recognising
>> something?
>> 4. the difference between the worldly view and the absolute view
>> 5. What is the unique method of Vedanta by which one attains liberation
>> from ignorance?
>> 6. the place of scriptures, reasoning and experience in the Vedantic
>> teachings. English with German translation.
