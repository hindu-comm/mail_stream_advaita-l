Namaste Bhaskar Ji,

Reg  // mAya is the shakti through which brahman become creator, without
which brahman is incapable of doing anything.  Hence it would be better to
go by siddhAnta that shakti or shakta is Shuddha brahman only //,

I am not sure I understood  this correctly. In the first sentence you are
saying mAyA is the shakti. In the second sentence you are saying  ** shakti
or shakta is Shuddha brahman only **. The two appear to me to be
contradictory. In my understanding, in the first sentence, mAyA should be
stated as  the **entity** and not **shakti**. That it is a **shakti** is
adhyAsa only. I am not sure if you have meant the same.
Regards
