Namaste Jaishankar,
//I don't have any doubts in the knowledge which was handed down to me by
my Guru.//
Quite understandable and solid for a new student but closed-mindedness is
the defect of such a position. Our tradition was founded on arguments such
that we would not be entitled to conviction without argument. One needs to
be careful to avoid vitanda - manana and samyak jnana need to be our
effort.

Here is a re-post of an unbiased, non-sectarian, non-polemic etic view on
some of the issues SSSS raises. It comes as a series of scholarly
confirmations. I think it is a necessary read and reflection for all
jijnasu-s. I hope you agree.
Regards, Michael Chandra
