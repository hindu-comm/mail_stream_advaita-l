**Crucial note:** The *Bhashya* explicitly states *no adjuncts exist in deep sleep*. Later commentators wrongly claim ignorance (*Avidya*) persists as an adjunct, preventing full unity with Brahman. This contradicts the scriptures.
praNAms
Hare Krishna

They not only contradict the scriptures they also contradict the bhAshyakAra.  I was totally surprised to see absolute silence and some members’ appreciation of the discovery that there is mithyA vastu darshana in sushupti.  Just because it is logically proved 😊 OTOH, shruti and bhAshyakAra clarifies : the individual jeeva who is in deep sleep has become pure consciousness without the second.  He does not see anything because the second thing to see does not exist although he is capable of seeing.  And here in this state the individual jeeva enters his OWN real atman i.e. para brahman becomes free from body and senses and thus becomes happy.  akartA sukhi bhavati because Atma svam AtmAnAm “paraM brahma” pravishya vimuktha karyakAraNasaMghAthaH..

There are aplenty of shruti and bhAshya vAkya-s like this and they some how found some erroneous conclusions by brushing all these under mUlAvidyA carpet and declaring it is NOT upAdhi rahita sthiti but upAdhi upashAnta sthiti, though it is like that (upAdhi upashAnta sthiti) there is no problem in accepting oneness in sushupti😊
