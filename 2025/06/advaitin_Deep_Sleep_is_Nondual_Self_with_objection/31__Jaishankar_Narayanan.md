In addition to what Subbu ji has mentioned the following clearly point out
that Sushupti is not what is claimed by SSSS and his followers.

1. Bhashyakara clearly talks about a kArana-sharIra in Ishavasya Upanishad
Bhasya for the 8th mantra

 शुद्धं निर्मलमविद्यामलरहितमिति कारणशरीरप्रतिषेधः ।
 śuddhaṃ nirmalamavidyāmalarahitamiti kāraṇaśarīrapratiṣedhaḥ ।
Pure, without any taint/dirt, without the taint of avidyA/ignorance, the
kArana-sharIra (causal body) negation

2. In Brhadaranyaka 4.4.34 Bhashya it is clearly stated that sleep is only
a drshtAnta (analogy) for moksha and the next prakarana is started to show
moksha which is the daarshtaantika (that which is revealed through an
analogy).

तत्र च बुद्धान्तात् स्वप्नान्तरम् अयमात्मा अनुप्रवेशितः ; तस्मात्
सम्प्रसादस्थानं मोक्षदृष्टान्तभूतम् ; ततः प्राच्यव्य बुद्धान्ते
संसारव्यवहारः प्रदर्शयितव्य इति तेन अस्य सम्बन्धः
tatra ca buddhāntāt svapnāntaram ayamātmā anupraveśitaḥ ; tasmāt
samprasādasthānaṃ mokṣadṛṣṭāntabhūtam ; tataḥ prācyavya buddhānte
saṃsāravyavahāraḥ pradarśayitavya iti tena asya sambandhaḥ

3. If sleep has no difference with moksha why even call it a state of
experience and talk about atmasvarUpa as turIya. Instead mAndukya
Upandishad should have named Atma as tritIya.

I have not seen proper answer for these from SSSS followers. They keep
repeating quotations which shows the similarity of sleep with moksha as
though it answers the above questions.
