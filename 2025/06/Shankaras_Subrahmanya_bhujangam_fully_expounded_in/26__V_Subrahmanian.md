Listening to the series is a great experience. We get to hear nuances of
Tamil, Sanskrit and even English poetry, many valuable incidents,
anecdotes, interesting cross references filled with bhakti and values for
life. He is someone who does not need a book for his long Ramayana,
Bhagavatam and other discourses. So with the Rama bhujangam, Subrahmanya
Bhujangam and many works of Bhagavatpada. There seems to be no limit to the
texts in his memory.
