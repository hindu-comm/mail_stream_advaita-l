A sacred opportunity is being offered to aastikas to participate in a
Saptaha of Jagadguru Namanam, Harikatha & Vedanta Shravana at Sringeri,
from 16th to 22nd June 2025 in English.
- Enlightening Upanyasa Saptaha on the divine life of Jagadguru Sri Sri
Abhinava Vidyatirtha Mahaswamiji will be delivered by Gurubhakta Mani Sri
N. Venkataraman Ji.
- Discourses on Lalita Sahasranama, highlighting several names of the
Divine Mother that embody profound Vedantic principles, will be delivered
by esteemed Vidwans.
- Harikatha Saptaaha on Shanmatha-Sthaapana-Acharya Jagadguru Sri Sri
Chandrashekhara Bharati Mahaswamiji will be rendered by Smt. Sharada
Gannavaram
Entry is free however, prior registration is mandatory. Math accommodation
is available for early entries on a first-come, first-served basis. Further
details & registration link: tinyurl.com/Sringeri
