Namaste Subbu ji

I would like to add that in the 13 chapter of the Geeta Bhagwan says that
> the witness of the body is kshetratrajna.
>

I think when Bhagvan says this, He means jIva and not witness. So, He has
kartA, bhOkta, jIva as the meaning of kshetrajna-1.

Immediately He says that that witness in every body is Me that is bramhan.
>

So, it is like tat tvam asi. Tat is kshetrajna-2 (Ishwara/sAkshI) and
kshetrajna-1 is jIva.

Regards.
Sudhanshu Shekhar.
