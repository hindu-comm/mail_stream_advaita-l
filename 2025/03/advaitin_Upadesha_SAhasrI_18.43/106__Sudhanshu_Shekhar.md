Hare Krishna Bhaskar prabhu ji.

Is this sAkshi for which ajnAna / avidyA is Vishaya something different
> from avidyAkruta sAkshi??
>

SAkshI is only one. And that is defined as avidyA-upahita-chaitanya.
Meaning in simple terms, chaitanya described from the frame of reference of
avidyA.

SAkshI is not avidyA-krita. SAkshi-tva is avidyA-krita.

Regards.
Sudhanshu Shekhar.
