Vedanta Shravana | Essential Vedanta


This Event is part of the discourse series exploring the foundational
principles of Vedanta and offering comprehensive guidance to spiritual
seekers. It is organised on account of the Vajrotsava Bharati Mahotsava,
commemorating the 75th Vardhanti of Jagadguru Shankaracharya Sri Sri
Bharati Tirtha Mahasannidhanam of Dakshinamnaya Sri Sharada Peetham,
Sringeri.



Check the Agenda section for complete details of the sessions and topics to
be covered.


Participants can extend their spiritual journey with the blessings of the
Sringeri Guru Parampara and delve into progressively profound teachings of
Bhagavatpada Sri Adi Shankaracharya.


Date and time:

Saturday & Sunday, 15th - 16th November 2025, 9:00a.m. - 3:00p.m


Venue:

Hindu Temple of Florida

5509 Lynn Rd, FL, USA, Tampa, Florida - 33624



   - Entry is free however prior registration is mandatory.
   - Move to the Admission Pass section below, and complete your
   registration.
   - All communication shall primarily be via WhatsApp. So kindly provide
   your WhatsApp number only.
   - Sessions will be conducted by distinguished speakers who possess
   profound knowledge of the Shastras, and they also exemplify the integration
   of spiritual wisdom and the demands of modern professional life, including
   worldly education and thriving corporate careers.
   - We encourage your active participation & attention during the sessions
   and hence recordings will NOT be shared.


Voluntary contributions are welcome on the day.

Need more information? Got Questions? Write to events at ahambrahmaasmi.org.


Aham Brahmaasmi Foundation® (A Unit of Dakshinamnaya Sri Sharada Peetham,
Sringeri) is envisioned with the mission to acquaint seekers worldwide with
the Upanishadic philosophy of Advaita as expounded by Jagadguru Sri Adi
Shankaracharya Bhagavatpada. The foundation is functioning with the
blessings of Sringeri Jagadguru Shankaracharya Sri Sri Bharati Tirtha
Mahasannidhanam and conducts its Vedanta-based programs with the direct and
divine guidance of Jagadguru Sri Sri Vidhushekhara Bharati Sannidhanam.
