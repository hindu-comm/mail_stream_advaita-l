SSSP USA established to facilitate donations in USD for directly supporting the activities of the Peetham

November 1, 2025

With the divine blessings of the Ubhaya Jagadgurus, the Sri Sringeri Sharada Peetham of USA (SSSP USA) - a new non-profit organization - has been
established to enable devotees in the United States to conveniently donate or transfer funds in US Dollars directly for their intended purposes.

Kindly note that to support the Peetham’s activities and events in the United States, devotees may continue to contribute to the Peetham's affiliate SVBF.
The SSSP USA setup is meant for directly supporting the activities of the Peetham in India.

Aastikas may visit the SSSP USA website at http://www.sringeriusa.org to learn about the donation options and to send in contributions.
