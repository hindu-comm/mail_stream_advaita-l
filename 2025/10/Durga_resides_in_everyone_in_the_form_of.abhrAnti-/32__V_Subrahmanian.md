> In the Durga Saptashati we have a section called 'aparājitā stotram' where
> many verses occur which have all words in common except for one particular
> word.
>
> This is one among them:
>
> Ya Devi sarva bhuteshu Bhrānti-rupena samsthitha ; Namastasye Namastasye
> Namastasye Namo Namaha.
>
> The Mother Divine is present as confusion and delusion in every being..
>
> One can read the full stotram with meaning in English here:
> https://www.artofliving.org/wisdom/wssst/significance-of-devi-stotra
>
> The final message is: every trait, positive or otherwise, is a
> manifestation of that Supreme Power. These powers are actually expressions
> of the three guna-s Sattva, Rajas and Tamas. It is with viveka,
> discrimination, that one has to handle them in one's efforts to achieve his
> goals.
>
> Here one can read the stotram in many language scripts
