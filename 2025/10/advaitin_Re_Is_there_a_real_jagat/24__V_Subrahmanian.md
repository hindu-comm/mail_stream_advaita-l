Bhagavatam says 'the creation (world) is an appearance in Brahman: yatra
trisargo mRShA.

And we have already seen the Bhagavatam gives the rope snake analogy to the
world and also has the clay-pot, etc examples for the Advaitic idea of non
difference of cause and effect.
