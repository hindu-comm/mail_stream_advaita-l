Hari Om,

kAraNa-tva is proved to be anirvachanIya in KhanDana-khanDa-khAdya by
refuting eight descriptions posed by NyAya. The anirvachanIyatA of
kAraNa-tva implies the mithyAtva thereof. And hence all belief in reality
of cause-hood/causal character/causation is liable to be ignored being
illusory. And one situates as one's own Self.

A write-up on rejection of causation as per KhanDana-khanDa-khAdya can be
downloaded from:
