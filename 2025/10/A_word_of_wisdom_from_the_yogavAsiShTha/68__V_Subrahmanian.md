भारोऽविवेकिनः शास्त्रं भारो ज्ञानं च रागिणः।
अशान्तस्य मनो भारो भारोऽनात्मविदो वपुः॥

योगवासिष्ठ:

Scriptural knowledge is a burden for one who lacks discrimination,
spiritual wisdom is a load for a passionate person, the mind is a burden
for one who is restless and lacks control and the body itself is a burden
to one devoid of knowledge of the Self.

Yogavāsiṣṭha
