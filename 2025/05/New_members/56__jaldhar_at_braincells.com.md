Mridula Venkatesh
-----------------

Hello all,

There are people you look at and at a glance, the desire to know everything
about them erupts abruptly. I, however, consider myself not to be a person
of such great stature and shall thereby refrain from divulging much about
myself and more about what brings me here.

I consider my interest in Advaita-Vedanta to be fated in many ways. I was
born into a family that practices advaita-vedanta, however, like most people
my age, I found myself looking elsewhere to find answers. A couple of years
later, I find myself back where I started, and this time wish to explore and
understand the beauty of advaita-vedanta.
