With the Benign Blessings of Sringeri Shankaracharyas

Sri Ramnath Bhagavath is happy to share "Advaita Pancharatnam" with you all
today, on the auspicious day of Shri Shankara Jayanthi.
Advaita Pancharatnam is a small yet profound poem by Adi Shankara, that
speaks the essence of Advaita.

Hamsa (The Swan) being the symbol of Advaita, the rendition have tuned and
sung in pancha hamsa ragas:

(1) Hamsanandi
(2) Hamsadhwani
(3)Hamsanadam
(4)Hamsadeepika and
(5)Hamsavinodini.

Sri  A.S Ram has wonderfully done the musical orchestration and
arrangements.
