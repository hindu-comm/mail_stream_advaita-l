> Please refer to the translation of prAdhAnika rahasya which is an appendix

> Translation of this text has been shared by Shri Jaldhar many years ago:
>
> https://www.advaita-vedanta.org/archives/advaita-l/2009-September/022572.html
>
> Three avatAra-s of Adi mAtA canDikA/ durgA - each created two children
> similar in guNa-s to
> the durgA avatAra:
> - mahA kAli with dominance of tamas crated: rudra and sarasvati
> - mahA lakShmi with dominance of rajas created : brahmA and lakShmi
> - mahA sarasvati with dominance of sattva created : viShNu and gauri
>
> Please note, that there is no suggestion here regarding one guNa being
> superior or one
> guNa being inferior. Each guNa is critical to creation.
>
