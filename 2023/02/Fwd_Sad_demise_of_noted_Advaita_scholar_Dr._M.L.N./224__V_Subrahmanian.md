Very sad to convey that our dear Dr. M.L.N.Murthy passed away suddenly
following a massive heart attack this evening at Sringeri. RIP.
Dr. M L Narasimha Murthy served at the Rashtriya Sanskrit
Vidyapeetha(National Sanskrit University, Tirupati) as Vice Chancellor &
Senior Professor of Advaita Vedanta. He holds a Doctorate in Adwaita
Philosophy (Mahavakyartha Vicara). He is well versed in Sanskrit, Hindi,
Kannada, Telugu, Malayalam and English languages. He has authored several books
in Sanskrit & English besides gracing many Vakyartha Vidwat Sabhas across
the country.
