प्राणं चेति ।

By the word 'prana' in the Bhashya we aught to take Agni, the Bhokta, on
the basis of the Bh.Gita 15.14 verse:  I, as, the fire cook the food in
everyone's stomach'.  Since agni is connected with prana, agni, bhokta is
indicated by the word prana.
