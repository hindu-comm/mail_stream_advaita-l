Yes Suresh ji, in the scheme of Shanmata, six deities, those who hold any
of these as their ishta devata, consider that devata to be the
jagatkaaranam Brahman and that they are vishwarupa. There are puranic
descriptions of the vishwa rupa of these deities.

The 'namaH savitrE jagadEka-chakShuShE jagat prasUti sthithi nAsha
hEtave...virinchi narayana shankarAtmanE' addressed to Surya in our TrikAla
SandhyAvandanam says Surya is the Jagat kAraNam. That way the Jagat is his
RUpa, vishvarUpa.  In various Puranas Skanda is stated to be the
JagatkAraNam. The Ganesha purana holds that the Trimurtis have their origin
in that God. There is a Surya shatakam which holds that the Trimurtis are
actually Surya. The Skanda Purana holds that Skanda is the Creator. Here is
a Kannada article full of citations on Skanda is Para Brahman:
https://adbhutam.files.wordpress.com/2021/03/f-skanda-is-brahman-kan.pdf

Also, the Mundakopanishad says: A Brahmajnani when prayed for Moksha, the
aspirant will get it. There the Jnani is stated to be 'one in whom the
world/creatoin rests.'
