>
> In fact, if we accept bheda between two forms of Ishvara in all respects
> in vyavahAra then it is as good as saying there are two Ishvara-s, which
> would be absurd. But to avoid that we do not have to admit there is atyanta
> abheda between two forms of Ishvara in vyavahAra either. Nor do we have to
> say only one of the two is Ishvara either, as vishiShTAdvaita / dvaita say.
>
> We instead say both Shiva and Vishnu are Ishvara only, they have just
> taken different names and forms for our benefit.
>
Namaste sir.
Yes this is the view of sampradaya. The distinction of forms of isvara is
only different in forms and names. Not in their isvartva.
