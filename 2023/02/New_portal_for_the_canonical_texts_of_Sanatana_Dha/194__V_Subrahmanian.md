With a view to promoting / supporting the conservation / preservation of
traditional knowledge by preserving, maintaining and providing access to
Indian traditional knowledge systems, in general, and the foundations of
Sanatana Dharma, in particular, Sanatana Sampatti Foundation ® was founded
in June 2022.
One of the first principal activities is to develop and maintain a
state-of-the-Art portal, Sanatana.in, hosting the foundational sources of
Sanatana Dharma, thereby sharing this timeless wisdom and making it
accessible to everyone. This portal will be developed under the direct
supervision and guidance of Jagadguru Sri Shankaracharya of Dakshinamnaya
Sri Sharadapeetham, Sringeri.
The portal will host the following:
• the four Vedas (along with recitation of available shaakaas / paathas),
along with
Vedangas and traditional commentaries - Sayanacharya and Bhatta Bhaskara;
• Vedanta sources - Upanishads, Brahmasutras;
• Smriti texts - Mahabharata (with Nilakanteeya commentary), Bhagavata (with
Sridhariya commentary), Ramayana (with Tilaka commentary), and all Puranas ;
• Dharmashastra texts as organised in the magnificent volumes, History of
Dharma
Shastra by Mahamahopadhyaya P. V. Kane.
