Q21. किं गहनं?
What is difficult to understand?
A21. स्त्रीचरितं |
The activities of women.


Q22. कश्चतुरो?
Who is intelligent?
A22. यो न खण्डितस्तेन ।
One who is not confused by them.


Q23. को दु:खं
What is unhappiness?
A23. असंतोषः |
Inability to get satisfaction.


Q24. किं लाघवं?
What is cause for dishonor?
A24. अधमतो याच्ञा |
Asking for favors from low class people.


Q25. किं जीवितं?
What is life?
A25. अनवद्यं |
That which is immaculate.


Q26. किं जाड्यं?
What is dullness?
A26. पाठतॊप्यनभ्यासः।
Not repeating or practicing what we have learned.


Q27. को जागर्ति?
Who is awakened?
A27. विवेकी |
One who is able to discriminate.


Q28. का निद्रा?
What is sleep?
A28. मूढता जन्तोः |
The stupidity of people.
