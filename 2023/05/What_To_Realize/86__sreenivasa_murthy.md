Dear Friends, AUM || AtmA vAA idamagramAsIT || Aitareya 1-1-1In the beginning all this verity was Atman.AtmatO idagaM sarvam || Chandogtya 7-26-1 All this springs from Atman.ahamEvEdagM sarvam || Chandogya 7-25-1
        I, indeed, am all this.
AtmaivEdagM sarvam || Chandogya 7-25-2 Atman, indeed is all this.The one word Self is equivalent to the mind, body, man, individual, the supreme and all else. [Sri Bhagavan; Talks with Sri Ramana ; Dialogue 238]It is this that a student of Vedanta has to cognize / realize within himself by himself to be an Atmavit. All one's reading, manana and nididhyAsana should be centred around this only.
With respectful pranams,Sreenivasa Murthy
