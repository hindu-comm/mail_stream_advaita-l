Reg  //  What is the position in Advaita regarding swarUpa of abhAva?  //

And

      // Here, what do we hold --

Pot-abhAva is floor.

Pot-abhAva is a distinct vastu than floor //

Position of Advaita is that Pot is only an appearance. So is the Floor.
Only an appearance. Both are not real. The ONLY  Realty or **vastu** is the
Ultimate Reality, Atman/Brahman which is  सर्व प्रत्यय वेद्य (sarva
pratyaya vedya).

You may like to refer to an interesting article on the topic of AbhAva by
Prof Hiriyanna available in the following text
