(on Ramanujacharya's sapta vidha anupapatti with regard to the Avidya of
Advaita)

 In this speech, marked in the video, from 1 hour.58 minutes onwards,  the
eminent Vidwan Sri Mani Dravid ShastrinaH explains how ignorance, ajnana,
of anything, cannot be 'abhava rupa/jnana abhava' but by default has to be
bhava rupa alone. He goes on to give evidence from the Upanishad, day to
day experiences and demonstrates that by the pramanas such as prathyaksha,
anumana, arthapatti and shabda - all of them proving the bhava rupatva
alone.
