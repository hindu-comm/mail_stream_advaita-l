In the Ramayana tatparya sara sangraha stotram Sri Appayya Dikshitar cites
a few verses from the Adhyatma Ramayana, by naming the text.

Appayya Dikshitar's timeline is:  1520–1593 CE.

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adhyatma_Ramayana

*Adhyatma Ramayana* (Devanāgarī
<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devan%C4%81gar%C4%AB>: अध्यात्म रामायण, IAST
<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Alphabet_of_Sanskrit_Transliteration>
: *Adhyātma Rāmāyaṇa*, lit. 'Spiritual Ramayana') is a 13th- to
15th-century Sanskrit <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanskrit> text that
allegorically interprets the story of Hindu epic *Ramayana
<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramayana>* in the Advaita Vedanta
<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advaita_Vedanta> framework.[1]
