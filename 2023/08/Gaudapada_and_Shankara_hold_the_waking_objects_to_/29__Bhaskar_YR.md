Do you agree that the appearance of the world continues for the jnAni?


  *   Yes prabhuji, it will continue to appear.  And if I continue, for the jnAni jagat is sarvaM and brahma mayaM  as a result of his paripUrNa samyakjnAna / sarvAtmabhAva whereas for the ajnAni jagat is asarvaM/aparipurNaM/abrahmam due to his avidyA parichinna drushti or due to qualified knowledge about jagat.

Do you agree he has a body after jnAna?


  *   Yes, as an ajnAni I cannot see beyond the body, mind and intellect of the jnAni tend to see jnAni’s body, hence Sri Shankara bhagavatpAda is a jnAni,  ramaNa maharshi at Arunachala another jnAni, Sri chadrashekharendra Saraswati of Kanchi as yet another jnAni, HH Sri Chandrashekhara Bharati is one more jewel in brahmajnAni’s big list.  Infact there is list of brahma jnAni-s in shruti itself by their names.  So in the view of ajnAni the jnAni is definitely embodied.  But what about the jnAni’s realization about his own (individual) body??  If he thinks he is body (dehAtma buddhi) then is it not called avidyaka prabhuji??  So body of the jnAni after jnana should be understood as per the jnAni’s verdict.  1-1-4 bhAshya vAkya is relevant here and in light of that we have to contextually understand jnAni’s sva-hrudaya pratyaya jnana, continuation of his organs / indriya-s etc. even after Samyak jnana (the fourth adhyAya in sUtra where shankara talks about jnAni’s individuality and his identification with his own hrudaya/mana) .


Do you agree that a jnAni is completely free of any bandha after jnAna?


Ø     Yes he is avidyA vinirmukta, he does not have even an iota of avidyA.  It is because of the simple fact both vidyA and avidyA cannot take the same place at same time in the buddhi of brahma jnAni.  Moreover, if the socalled jnAni having even tip of the hair avidyA his jnana is not paripUrNa.  jnAni is no more an individual jnAni he is brahman only, brahmavit brahmaiva bhavati, and this is not after the physical death of the jnAni.
