>  coming back to adhyAsa as I said as per Sri SSS, adhyAsa is misconception (vipareeta grahaNa) NOT jnAnAbhAva (agrahaNa) He more than one place emphasizes that this is predominantly insisted by shankara in all through PTB.  adhyAsa according to him jnAnAdhyAsa only thinking about him kartru-bhOktru with dehAdi saMghAta adhyAsa etc. due to this adhyAsa adhyArOpa and adhyArOpita takes place.  Wrongly seeing rope (adhyAsa) thinking it as snake (adhyArOpa) seeing the snake (adhyArOpita).  At one place he gives the example of just born child which naturally comes with dehAdhyAsa when someone hurts its body it will start crying.  So, this adhyAsa, in the form of taking one thing for another, AtmAnAtma adhyAsa is the main cause and eradication of this is the primary purpose of all loukika and vaidika vyavahAra (adhyAsa bhAshya says this as you know).  To substantiate this type of adhyAsa which is avidyA he often quotes adhyAsa bhAshya vAkya : evamayaM anAdiH anantaH naisargika adhyAsaH mithyA pratyaya rUpaH ( from this mithyA pratyaya he reiterates vidyA-avidyA is just antaHkaraNa dOsha, not belonging to brahman) katrutvabhOktrutva pravartakaH sarvaloka pratyakshaH, ahamidaM mamedamiti naisargikOyaM lOka vyavahAraH. anyOnyadharmAmschAdhyAsa itaretaraavivekena etc.  And the point to be noted here as per Sri SSS, mithyAjnAna nimittaH is NOT ( am emphatic NO indeed)  mithyA ajnAna (which is having a shakti having the powers like AvaraNa, vikshepa, which is also anirvachaneeya, brahmAshrita bhAvarUpa etc.) and this nimitta does not mean material cause (upAdAna kAraNa) for the adhyAsa.
