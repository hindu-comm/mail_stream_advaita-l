Namaste,

Aarsha Vidya Varshini has organised a Vedanta Retreat from 28th Dec 2023 to
6th Jan 2024 at Pujyasri Omkarananda Mahaswamigal’s SRI SWAMI
CHIDBHAVANANDA  ASHRAMAM, Chatrapatti Road,  Vedapuri - Theni, Tamil Nadu,
India.

You are welcome to join Acharyas Kalyankumar and Jaishankar on a journey of
Self Discovery on the banks of Surabhi Saraswati Nadi (Mullaiperiyar
River), where self knowledge has been flowing for the benefit of all who
seek. You can register at http://www.aarshavidyavarshini.org/register.html

We will have only 60 participants in the retreat. So confirm your
participation early. You can send a message to +91 9710946652 or email to
info at aarshavidyavarshini.org for any info about the retreat.

Retreat Details
The retreat starts on Thursday 28th Dec 2023 at 11.30 AM with an
Orientation class for the participants. The retreat ends with lunch on
Saturday 6th Jan 2024.
The following texts will be taught in English during the retreat:
1. Kena Upanishad
2. Bhagavad Gita Chapter 2
3. Apastambha Sutra - AdhyAtma Patala


During the retreat, one afternoon will be for group visit to near by
temples in  and around Theni and one afternoon will be for going around
famous places like Thekkadi etc. depending on the weather conditions.

The participants should strictly follow vegetarian diet in the ashram. Any
use of prohibited items are not permitted in the ashram premises. No pet
animals are allowed inside the Ashram. Traditional dress should be worn in
the Ashram. Shorts or similar dress is not allowed. Ashram reserves the
right to ask any participant to vacate the room if there is any violation
of the above rules.


The participants can note that the Vedanta teaching is given free for all
in keeping with the tradition. However participants are expected to donate
a reasonable amount (yatha shakti in keeping with your means ) to cover the
cost of their stay and food.


For more details about Ashram rules and regulations including dress code,
please check the below url
https://www.vedaneri.org/accomodation

Om.
