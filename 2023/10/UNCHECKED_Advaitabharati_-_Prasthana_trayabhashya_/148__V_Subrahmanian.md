 In a Srimukham to a Tamil translation of Gitabhashya, Jagadguru Sri
Bharati Tirtha Mahasannidhanam says

अनाघ्रातसुरभारतीगन्धा अपि भाष्यमिदमवबुध्यन्तामिति धिया प्रान्तीयभाषास्वपि
भाष्यस्यास्य अनुवादो विहितः।

This has motivated  Sanatana Sampatti to start अद्वैतभारती under which SSF
will aim at bringing translations of Bhagavatpada's prasthanatraya bhashyas
into Indian languages. As a first offering, Jagadguru Sri Vidhushekhara
Bharati Sannidhanam launched the Advaitabharati.sanatanasampatti.in portal
on 26.10.2023, Thursday, in Sringeri.
