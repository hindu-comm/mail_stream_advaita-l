Q77. किं लघुतायाः मूलं?
What is the cause of disgrace?
A77. प्राकृतपुरुषेषुयाच्ञा ।
Running after materialists to get favors from them.


Q78. रामादपि कः शूरः
Who is even more heroic than Lord Rama?
A78. स्मरशरनिहतो न यश्चलति |
One who does not waver even under the blows of Kandarpa’s (Kamadeva's or cupid's) arrows (of love).


Q79. किमहर्निशमनुचिन्त्यं?
What should we contemplate day and night?
A79. भगवच्चरणं न संसारः ।
The Lord’s lotus feet, and not this world’s material life.


Q80. चक्षुष्मन्तोऽप्यन्धाः के स्युः?
Who is blind, in spite of having good eyes?
A80. ये नास्तिका मनुजाः |
One who has no faith.
