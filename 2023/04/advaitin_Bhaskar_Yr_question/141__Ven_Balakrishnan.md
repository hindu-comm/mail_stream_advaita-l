If the mind-body-world ia a superimposition on consciousness, and this superimposition is equated to ignorance, then the removal of ignorance must mean the cessation of the superimposition. Hence deep sleep is used as the pointer to Brahman, realisation as the dissolution of particular consciousness (like a salt doll in water) and paramahansa sannyasa as the inevitable outcome of the non-action of a jnani. 

Ramanamaharishi in Ulladu Narpadu v5:
“If we scrutinise, the body is a form of five sheaths.  Without the body, does the world exist? Is there anyone who, having given up identification with the body, has seen the world?"
