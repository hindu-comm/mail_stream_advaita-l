According to the mantra 6-2 of Prashna Upanishad The Creatoris within you, within your body.Why don't you discover Him within you and then put the questionto Him. Then you will certainly get an authentic correct answer.
The answers by others will be only intellectual speculation.
Please ponder over.With respectful namaskars,Sreenivasa Murthy.

Isvara is said to be the creator,sustainer,destroyer etc of the universe.
He is also the karma phala data, etc. He is possessed of gunas.
He is the atman(antaryamin of all beings). But the antaryamin atman is
nirvesha and never does any activity. The atman by its mere presence
facilitates the body and manas do its work,
Then how is that isvara is the atman(nirvesha) at the same time controller
of maya and possesed of gunas of sarvashaktha,sarvajnata etc.
