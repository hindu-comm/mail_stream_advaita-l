Dear Subbuji,

There is a saying that "Om" is “rAmnAmnA samutpannaH”, i.e., the word “Om”

came from the name ‘Ram”. Here, Ahalya says the same  in the following verse
*:*


ॐकारवाच्यस्त्वं राम वाचामविषयः पुमान् ।

वाच्यवाचकभेदेन भवानेव जगन्मयः ॥ ११॥


My 2 cents
