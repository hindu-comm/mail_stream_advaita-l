Dear friends ,
Mumukshus who want to be Atmavits are requested to ponder overthe upadesha and become kRutArthAs / Atmavits.Quote:
Whena person assumes this point of view from which alone he can survey all theregions of life, Viz., waking, dreaming and sleep, he has risen to a a positionin which he is no longer the empirical ego.
Unquote.
It is so simple to become a Jnani or Atmavit 
if one applies these teaching to himself within himself.BruhadaraNyaka Sruti Says : AtmannEvAtmAnaM paSyati ||
This posting is from one who has found vast benefits 
from such upadeshas.
With respectful namaskars,Sreenivasa Murthy
