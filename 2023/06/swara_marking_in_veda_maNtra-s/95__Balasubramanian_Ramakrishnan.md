The word me is anudAtta if taken as a separate pada - you can refer to the
padapAtha. The general rule is a word may have no or one sometimes more
than one udAtta. The others are techically anudAtta-s to begin with and
will undergo transformation depending on what what svara comes before+after
it. So without going into all the details me can have low or middle svaras.
The middle svara is unmarked.

Ramakrishnan
