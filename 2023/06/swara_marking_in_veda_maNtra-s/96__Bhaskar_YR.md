But in saMhita pAtha if we can see all me do not have anudAtta some have anudAtta some without any swara.  Like for example from tilaaschame all me till the end of anuvAka does not have swara mark ( or all 'me' has middle swara only/no marking) and in krama pAtha ( shaMchame anuvAka) meSbhayam ( me abhayaM) meSnAmayat (me anAmayat) etc. me does not have any swara marking, I don't know whether it is printing mistake or rule is like that.  Hence I asked whether any rule is applicable in marking the svara-s so that we can ourselves determine whether swara marked in printed texts correct or not.

The word me is anudAtta if taken as a separate pada - you can refer to the padapAtha. The general rule is a word may have no or one sometimes more than one udAtta. The others are techically anudAtta-s to begin with and will undergo transformation depending on what what svara comes before+after it. So without going into all the details me can have low or middle svaras.
The middle svara is unmarked.
