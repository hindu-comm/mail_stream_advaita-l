Power to Resume Nityakarmas NOW

Why Now?

The Vedas are the primary source and foundational basis of our sanAtana
dharma.  वेदोऽखिलो धर्ममूलं declares the manusmRti - the entirety of the
Vedic corpus is the basis of dharma.

Therefore, the study of the vedAs in the prescribed manner from a guru, and
passing them on to the next generation, is the obligation of every dvija,
twice-born, to preserve the wisdom of the vedAs for all time.

In ancient times, students would perform the vedAdhyayana (ritualistic
study of the vedAs) after Brahmopadesam or Upanayana Samskaras, in the four
and half months of the year commencing on the full moon of the shravaNa
month. This commencement of the annual study of the vedAs on the prescribed
day is termed as upAkarma.

Unfortunately, Vedic learning has further been compromised to a significant
extent, with only a handful of brAhmaNas still engaged in this activity
critical to the preservation of the vedA-s, the foundational root of our
sanAtana dharma.

How can I Make Sandhyavandanam a Routine?

Due to stressful workaday life or tumultuous school life, the performance
of nityakarma of sandhyAvandanam - which is the most basic ritual in a
dvija's life - has declined alarmingly. The general thinking seems to be:
‘I am not alone in not doing my Nityakarma’.

This is something that is very much within our power to change.

SandhyAvandanam is a very simple ritual that hardly takes 10-15 minutes for
the practitioner and is something that all of us can certainly do, even in
the midst of our busy lives, if we put our minds to it. The benefits of the
regular performance of sandhyAvandanam are truly great and something that
is within the reach of each of our experience.

One challenge that is commonly encountered is that, not having performed
sandhyAvandanam for years, many amongst us may have forgotten the procedure
and mantras - perhaps some of us are too embarrassed to ask anyone to teach
them.

Further, youngsters and others may not be aware of the significance or
efficacy of the mantras and the meaning.
