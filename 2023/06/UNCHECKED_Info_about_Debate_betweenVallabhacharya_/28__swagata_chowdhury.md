Can you confirm the veracity of the purported discourse that transpired
between Vallabhacharya and Upendra? Pray tell, who emerged victorious and
who suffered defeat in the debate?
This information is drawn from the book "History of Advaita Chintarota
Itihasa" by Rajendranath Ghosh Vedantabhushan. However, scholars of the
Vallabh sect assert that such claims are wholly false.
