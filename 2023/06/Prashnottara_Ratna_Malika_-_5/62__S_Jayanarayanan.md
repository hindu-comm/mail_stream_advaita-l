Q29. नलिनीदलगतजलवत्तरलं किं?
What is temporary like water on a lotus leaf?
A29. यौवनं धनं चायुः।
Youth, wealth, health and life.


Q30. कथय पुनः के शशिनः किरणसमानाः?
Tell me again, what is similar to the moon rays?
A30. सज्जना एव |
Only good people.


Q31. १२. को नरकः?
What is hell?
A31. परवशता |
Being under someone else’s control.


Q32. किं सौख्यं?
What is well-being?
A32. सर्वसङ्गविरतिर्या।
Detachment from all kinds of contacts.


Q33. किं साध्यं
What should be done?
A33. भूतहितं |
The work for the benefit of creatures.


Q34. प्रियं च किं प्राणिनां?
What is dear to all creatures?
A34. असवः |
Life.
