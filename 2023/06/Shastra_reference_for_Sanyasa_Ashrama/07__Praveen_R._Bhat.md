Purvamimamsa that follows yAvajjIvechChruti also accepts sannyAsa for those
who are unable to do karma due to old age, lack of capacity/wealth, loss of
limbs, etc. There is a question that comes up on this list on sannyAsa to
those belonging to abrAhmaNavarNa; there has also been a discussion as to
whether women have adhikAra in sannyAsa, all of which anyway accepts
sannyAsavidhi in shAstras.
