You successfully sent this one.  Keep doing the same with any questions 
you have or thoughts you would like to share.  But do not just add to an 
existing topic (unless of course you are responding to that topic.)  Start 
a new email with advaita-l at lists.advaita-vedanta.org in the recepient 
field and a suitably descriptive subject in the subject field, write your 
message and press send.

except the exact parts you are responding to as there is a firm (but 
generous limit) to how much you can send.  Also don't send pictures, 
audio/video etc.  That gets automatically blocked by the server.
