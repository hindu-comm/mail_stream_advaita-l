There is no denying that there is indeed the exalted jIva involved in the
creation process. And being a jIva, he is one way or another always
subordinate to Ishvara. So this first born or kArya Brahman with the
blessings/grace of kAraNa brahman or Ishvara initiated sRShTi.

The question is regarding whether the stithi and laya processes are also
done in the same way by the mediacy of kArya Brahman. The answer seems to
be yes as I understand.

That means viShNu/shiva/Ishvara is not to be associated with any particular
guNa or guNas which would limit him to a role as sthithi-kAraka or
laya-kAraka. Instead it's valid to.hold based on the references given by
Subbu ji that the forms of Ishvara are not constrained by any guNa as
upAdhi. But that Ishvara under whose overlordship, the three guNas are
active, commands/governs the hiraNyagarbha to do all the three functions
viz , the creation, preservation and resolution of the universe.
