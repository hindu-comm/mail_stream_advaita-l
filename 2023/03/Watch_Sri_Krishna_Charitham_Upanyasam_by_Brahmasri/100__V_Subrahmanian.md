The discourses by Brahmasri Sundar Kumar are a class by themselves:  He
presents the Harikatha interspersed with many assorted compositions from a
wide range of Bhagavatas across varied traditions and languages. His
authority on the Bhagavatam, Narayaneeyam, Ramayanam is immense. He
includes songs from Srivaishnava sources, both Tamil and Sanskrit. Hindi
and Marathi songs along with Telugu and Kannada too are part of his
repertoire. For all this he needs no book or paper to refer to. So much is
there in his memory.

The conveying of Bhakti bhava is unmatched. The takeaway for the listener
is immeasurable.
