 Dear Sri Aravinda Rao,

You write :"So much invective in the group of advaitins."

Kindly refer to mantra 1-2-8 of Mundakopanishad which reads thus :

avidyAyAM antarE vartamAnAH 


 svayaM dhIrAH panDtaMmanyamAnAH |

jaMGanyamAnAh pariyantimUDhAH

 andhEnaiva nIyamAnA mahandhAH || 


You draw the conclusion.

With respectful namaskars,

Sreenivasa Murthy.
