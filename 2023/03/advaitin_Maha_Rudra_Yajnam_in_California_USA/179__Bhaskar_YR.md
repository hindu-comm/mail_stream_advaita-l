Hare Krishna

I really do appreciate your secular approach.  But there is certain vidhi, nishedha in vedic rituals.  For example in rudra hOma those who are sitting to offer oblation to yagneshwara should be able to chant shata rudreeya (both namakaM and chamakaM), they should do anga nyAsa-s, kara nyAsa-s as well and they should chant the mantra-s while offering the havi.  So in certain rituals wives would not be allowed to offer oblations to agni.  They can sit for saMkalpa, during prOkshaNa, pUrNAhuti ritual etc. but should not actively participate in Havana prakriya.  Just my thoughts.

Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!

Bhaskar YR

Great news. Great to see that so many ritwiks are available.

A small suggestion may be considered. Usually at the time of havanam several families (husband and wife) would be sitting, offering oblation into the homa-kunda. Some temples restrict this to one community only, leaving a bad taste in others. I persuaded my local temple authorities (in Hyderabad) to allow people from all sections so that such discontent is avoided. And also the temple gets more resources. This may be particularly important in view of the discrimination laws which are promoted in the state of California. Someone can even deliberately create a situation.
