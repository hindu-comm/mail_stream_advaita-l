What is APK?

>  APK is adhyAtma prakAsha kAryAlaya, Holenarsipura, Karnataka, the publisher of Sri SSS's works.  APK perspective means Sri Satchidaanandendra Saraswati Swamiji, (founder APK) has written commentaries on shankara's PT bhAshya without depending on later vyAkhyAnakAra-s vyAkhyAna.  Hence sometimes some socalled traditionalists would say APK prakriya, mattur prakriya, Sri SSS's view point or APK perspective are untraditional as it is not aligned with post shankara schools and whatever said in post shankara schools like bhAmati & vivaraNa and subsequent works from these two schools are more traditional and authentic 😊 As a matter of fact, Sri SSS promptly and religiously tried to represent shankara's mUla bhAshya since there were conflict of opinions within the traditional schools like bhAmati and vivaraNa prasthAna.
