ŚANKARA JAYANTI SPECIAL - ŚĀNKARA STOTRĀNI

Śrī Gurubhyo Namaha

CALLING OUT ALL DEVOTEES OF ĀDYA ŚANKĀRĀCHĀRYA GURU PARAMPARĀ.

Jagadguru Ādi Śankarācharya has blessed us all with innmunerable stotras.
These stotras help every individual to ascend the path of devotion and
tattvajnāna.

Now you may offer your renditions of the stotras composed by Śrī
Bhagavatpāda as Samparnam through Sringeri Gurubandhava channel.

The guidelines are as follows:

1. Entries are invited from those aged 12 years and above.

2. Both, individual and group recitations are permitted.

3. Only those entries with perfect pronunciation will be selected for
streaming on the channel.

4. Prior registration is required.

5. Link for registration: https://forms.gle/J1dfqipkEPCVJXZ56

5. In the case of multiple entries of the same stotra being received,
requests will be sent out to selected entries to change their selection.

Important Dates:

Last date for registration: Wednesday, 15/3/2023

Last date for submission of entry: Monday, 20/3/2023

Please forward your audio/video recording to us with a short bio.

Please email your entries to:
sringerigurubandhava at gmail.com

We will stream them all after placing them at the Lotus Feet of our
Jagadgurus.

Thanks and Pranams

Team Sringeri Gurubandhava
