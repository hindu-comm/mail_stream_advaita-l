Agreed. The upadhis are admitted to be mithya. But when is it so? Only
after jnana is attained and duality is seized. Acharya chandrashekhara
saraswati says forms of isvara are eternal and true till one attains jnana
in other words becomes a jivanmuktha. Till then as the bhasyakara says in
BSB bhasya isvara is the ruler and we are the ruled.
 But even in the case of jivanmukthas such as abhinava vidyatirtha swamin,
they had immense devotion to isvara roopas. Infact acharya quotes the
bagavatha purana and says that "jivanmukthas who have nothing to attain,
are eager to listen to the leelas of bhagavan. Such is his glory".
