HARI OM,

Though I am not a scholar, I would like to state my understanding.

There was a multiplicity of religious practices, which Shankaracharya tried to address through his teaching. However, it is also important to recognize that infinite cannot be comprehended by mind,. SO,  value of physical form was recognized by Shankara and established 6 forms to be worshiped, based on the principle that all these forms were the reflections of Brahman and in the belief of the essential oneness of all deities, and their conceptualization of the myriad deities of India as various manifestations of the one divine power, Brahman.



Best Regards/  VTCS

VTCS Rao, Dean L&T IPM
