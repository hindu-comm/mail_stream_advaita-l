A fundamental difference between Panchayatana Puja and Shanmata is to be
known: the former is a method of worship while the latter is about upasana
of one deity amongst the six considering that deity to be one's ishta
devata who is Jagatkaaranam. The fruit of the former is chittashuddhi,
etc., while the latter can take one to Brahmaloka and krama mukti.

The two are not to be mixed.
