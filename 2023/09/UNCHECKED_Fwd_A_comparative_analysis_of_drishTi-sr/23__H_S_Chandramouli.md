Namaste Sudhanshu Ji,

No.

4-25 to 27 are presentation in the kArika of the refutation of  SautrAntika
and VaibhAshika Systems of Buddhism by the YogAchAra System of Buddhism.
This refutation of those Systems is accepted in the Advaita SiddhAnta as
stated in the preamble to 4-28. No separate refutation of those Systems are
presented here in the kArika. But then the same line of argument as used by
the YogAchAra System is used now, in 4-28, to refute the YogAchAra System
itself  by the SiddhAntin. Thus what is cited as from 4-26, while having
been  accepted as refutation of the other Systems of Buddhism, is rejected
later in 4-28 from the standpoint of  Advaita Siddhanta.
