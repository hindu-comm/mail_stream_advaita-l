A quick observation for your perusal 😊

I have a query - the word "sat" used in ChhAndogya 6.2.1 सदेव सोम्येदमग्र आसीदेकमेवाद्वितीयम् refers to nirvishesha Brahman or ajnAna-vishishTa-Brahman?

>  it has to be parabrahman only otherwise ekamevAdvitiyam does not suit to ajnAna vishishta brahman 😊 Before talking about anything srushti prakriya, shruti talks about parabrahman only at the start which is second to none.  But however, shankara elsewhere clarifies whenever brahma-tattva associated with jagat srushti it is kArya brahman / upAsya / apara...it does not anyway mean brahman endowed with avidyA even before the creation and introduction of jeeva aspect 😊 

Similarly the merger into "sat" during sushupti spoken through सता सोम्य तदा सम्पन्नो भवति refers to which sat? Nirvishesh-Brahmam or ajnAna-vishishTa-Brahman?

>  See mAdUkya mantra 6-7 and bhAshya...
