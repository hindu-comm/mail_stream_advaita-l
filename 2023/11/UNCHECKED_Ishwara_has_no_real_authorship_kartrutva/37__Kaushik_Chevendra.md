Namaste sir.
 I think a valid upamana for isvara and maya is like a Jeevanmuktha  and
his body. A Jeevanmuktha definitely has control over his body but he
remains a mere witness to it at the same time. Isvara too controls his maya
but also remains a witness to it at the same time. This is why Krishna also
makes many statements such as “ I give them faith in whatever form they
worship “ or “ I throw those souls into demonic wombs”, “ I bring forth the
universe again” etc.

Namo Narayana

> Brahman created this world mentally and entered it. In a dream one feels as
> if he has done some or the other action but in reality he has no doership,
> kartrutva. *He is just a witness to the dream, while only the three gunas
> do all the creation. This is how it is in the present context of Ishwara
> creating. Paramatma only appears as the creator of the world. Indeed,
> Ishwara does nothing.*
> Brahman nirguna, nishkriya, passive. Brahman is the cause of the world only
> by Maya association. This causality is not true.
> Lord Krishna says in the Bhagavad Gita:
> *मयाध्यक्षेण प्रकृतिः सूयते सचराचरम् ।*

> While I am a mere witness, nature, Prakruti, creates this world of all
> beings moving and unmoving.
