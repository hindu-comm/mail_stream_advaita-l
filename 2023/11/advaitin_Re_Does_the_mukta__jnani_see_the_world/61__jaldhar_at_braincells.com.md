On Thu, 16 Nov 2023, Sudhanshu Shekhar via Advaita-l wrote:

> Namaste Raghav ji.
>
> jIvanmukti is denied by MS in Advaita-siddhi.
>
> It has been held by MS that there has never been any jnAnI so far as the
> world is being perceived presently.
>
> Both these assertion are in Eka-jIva-vAda vicharah of Advaita-siddhi.
>
> jIvanmukti is admissible in nAnA jIva vAda only which is SDV.
>
> It is not correct to hold jIvanmukti in DSV and ajAtivAda.
>


This doesn't sound right to me.  In Bhagavadgita Gudharthadipika Swami MS 
clearly endorses the idea of Jivanmukti.  I don't have time to give you 
the exact reference but IIRC he describes it as the culmination of 
Vedantic sadhana and quotes Yogavasishtha and Bhagavatapurana in support.

You may be reading too much into what he says in Advaitasiddhi.
