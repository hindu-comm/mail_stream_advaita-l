of Jagadguru Shankaracharya Sri Sri Bharati Tirtha Mahaswamiji and the
guidance of Sringeri Jagadguru Sri Sri Vidhushekhara Bharathi Sannidhanam.


   1.  Discover the path from bondage to liberation, as laid out by
   Bhagavatpada Adi Shankara and other Jivanmuktas.
   2. Gain insights into pure scriptural literature and the structured
   approach to studying them.
   3. Opportunity to resolve your spiritual and dharmic queries through
   in-depth Q&A sessions with knowledgeable scholars deeply rooted in the
   study of Shastras.
