>     Analysis of a Misleading post
>
>Recently, a gentleman had written a post in which he mentions that Goswami
>Tulsidas has condemned Advaita and that those who follow the philosophy of
>Advaita shall end up in Hell.

I highly doubt this.  When Tulsidasji wrote the Ramacharitamanas he came under criticism from certain Pandits of Kashi who felt such a sacred text as the Ramayana should not be translated into "mleccha bhasha".  It was Swami Madhusudana Saraswati acclaimed by all as the head of the Scholarly community in Kashi who came to his defense.  He even penned a shloka:

आनन्दकानने ह्यस्मि‌ंजंगमस्तुलसीतरुः।
कवितामंजरी भाती रामभ्रमरभूषिता ॥

"In Anandavan[1] there is a walking Tulsi tree whose poetry-buds are always frequented by the bee called Rama."

[1] another name for Kashi or specifically a forest on the opposite bank of the Ganga where even now many saints dwell.

Given that Swamiji is well known to have been an ardent defender of Advaita Vedanta against all comers does it seem plausible that he would have advocated for one who was an opponent?
