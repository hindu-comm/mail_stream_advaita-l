Namaste Subbuji
Thanks for sharing that. The article highlights clearly the fact that in
the Advaita tradition, the false binary of jnAna vs. bhakti does not exist;
therefore there is no locus to debate the superiority of one over the other.

The highest bhakta follows nididhyAsanam ( a.k.a. svasvarUpa-anusandhAnam)
as his particular way of relating to Ishvara - such practice being most
proximate to sAxAtkAra.

Om
Raghav
