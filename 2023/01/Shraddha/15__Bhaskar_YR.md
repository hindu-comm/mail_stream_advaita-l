Namaste, In South India the tithi vyApti in aparAhna is a must to decide on shraddha day.

>  Yes for the shrAddhAcharaNa,  aparAnha vyApti is important.  And to decide the tithi for the janana & maraNa, the tithi that is there at that time is considered.  Normally we the south Indians who follow Ontikoppal will observe sUryasiddhAnta and not vAkya.
