Sridhara Swamin cites the Naishkarmyasiddhi.  Pl. see the following
images.  Here is the English translation:

https://archive.org/details/naiskarmyasiddhi_201510/page/n117/mode/2up

It is nice to note Sureshwaracharya mentioning the 'was not, is not, will
not be' idea in the introduction to this verse in the Naish.siddhi which he
says in this Bri.Up.bh.vartika too:

तत्त्वमस्यादिवाक्योत्थसंयग्धीजन्ममात्रतः ।
*अविद्या सह कार्येण नासीदस्ति भविष्यति ॥ *

The Tattvamasi, etc. passages give rise to that knowledge which dispels the
avidya, along with its effects, which is non-existent in all the three
periods of time.
