कल्पयित्वाऽऽत्मना यावद् आभासमिदमीश्वरः ।
द्वैतं तावन्न विरमेत् ततो ह्यस्य विपर्ययः ॥ १० ॥
As long as one does not give up distinctions like 'this is a man, this is a
woman', then the superimposition of qualities in those bereft of qualities,
will continue. That is, there is a misunderstanding, viparaya, perceiving
one as the other. One has to let it go through acquiring right knowledge.
Otherwise, bondage will be a never-ending story.
This indicates that the idea of gender, male and female, is not present in
the true nature of the Atman. It is certainly a superimposition, a vision
of error, in the Atman.
