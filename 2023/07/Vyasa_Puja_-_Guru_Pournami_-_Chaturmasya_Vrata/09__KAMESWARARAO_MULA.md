Dear followers of Sanatana Dharma,

The 36th Peethadheeshwara of Dakshinamnaya Sri Sharada Peetham, Jagadguru Shankaracharya Sri Sri Bharati Tirtha Mahaswamiji will be observing His 49th Chaturmasyam in the Shobhakrit Samvatsara along with His successor-designate, Jagadguru Sri Sri Vidhushekhara Bharati Sannidhanam, in Sringeri.

The Jagadguru will commence the Chaturmasya Vrata with Vyasa Puja on Ashadha Poornima (Monday, the 3rd of July 2023) and complete it on Bhadrapada Poornima (Friday, the 29th of September, 2023).
