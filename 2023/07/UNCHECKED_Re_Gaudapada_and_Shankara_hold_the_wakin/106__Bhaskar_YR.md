Anyway since you are particular about it, reference may be made to BSB 2-1-14 on sUtra  ** तदनन्यत्वमारम्भणशब्दादिभ्यः ** (tadananyatvamArambhaNashabdAdibhyaH).  My understanding of  अनन्यत्वम्  (ananyatvam)  is that the relationship between Brahman and Creation is one of  कार्यकारण विलक्षण  (kAryakAraNa vilakShaNa).  Not one of  कार्यकारण सम्बन्ध (kAryakAraNa sambandha).  Thus Creation is a composite of AdhisththAna and  Adhyastha. The two enjoy different levels of Reality (विषमसत्ता) (viShamasattA).  They are the two  parts I was referring to.
