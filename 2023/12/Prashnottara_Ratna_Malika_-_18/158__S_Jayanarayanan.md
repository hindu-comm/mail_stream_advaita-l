Q94. किं शस्त्रं सर्वेषां?
What is the supreme weapon?
A94. युक्तिः |
Reasoning.


Q95. माता च का?
Who is the mother of all?
A95. धेनु:।
The cow.


Q96. किं नु बलम्?
What is real strength?
A96. यद्धैर्यं |
Courage.


Q97. को मृत्युः?
What is death?
A97. यदवधारणारहितत्वं |
Carelessness.
