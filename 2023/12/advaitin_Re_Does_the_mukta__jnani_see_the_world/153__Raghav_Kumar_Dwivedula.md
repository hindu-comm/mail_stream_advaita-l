Namaste Srinath ji
As you know, in the vaitathya prakaraNam, we have the prasiddha hetu,
dRshyatvAt mithyAtvam etc., with the shloka "vaitathyam sarvabhAvAnAm
svapna AhurmanIshinaH".

Would you agree that, that yukti of Sri Gaudapada, establishing the
ontological equivalence of waking and dream, is sound?
