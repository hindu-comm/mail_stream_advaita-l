These fall in the category of kshetram: icchaa dveshah sukham
duhkham...which are all vishaya to the vishayi the sakshi. All ghaTa jnana,
specific vishayaka ajnana, samshaya, etc. too are sAkshi pratyaksha and
have their locus in the antahkarana/manas. Being part of the concocted
world, they too are concoctions.

 कामः सङ्कल्पो विचिकित्सा श्रद्धाश्रद्धा
धृतिरधृतिर्ह्रीर्धीर्भीरित्येतत्सर्वं मन एव   ...This list, not exhaustive,
is termed mind itself since they occur in the mind.  Brihadaranyaka
upanishad.
