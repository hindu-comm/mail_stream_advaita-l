Reg  //  These fall in the category of kshetram: icchaa dveshah sukham
duhkham...which are all vishaya to the vishayi the sakshi //

and

// कामः सङ्कल्पो विचिकित्सा श्रद्धाश्रद्धा
धृतिरधृतिर्ह्रीर्धीर्भीरित्येतत्सर्वं मन एव  //

These are said to be the attributes of the ego and they become vishaya to
the vishayi the sAkshi  only when there is their superimposition on the
ego. Not otherwise.
