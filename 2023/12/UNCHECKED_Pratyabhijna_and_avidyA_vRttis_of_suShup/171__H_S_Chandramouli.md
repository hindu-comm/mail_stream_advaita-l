Illumination by sAkshi leads to registering the contents of the vritti,
whether avidyA vritti or antahkaraNa vritti,  either in antahkaraNa if it
is active or in avidyA itself if antahkaraNa is in bIja avasthA. However,
since avidyA is the kAraNAvastha for the antahkaraNa, whatever contents of
the vritti  were registered in avidyA during the bIja avasthA of the
antahkaraNa is available for the antahkaraNa when it becomes active again.
